# FW2.6 (TE 2.6) : The company maintains a closed gender wage gap, reduces the gap, or justifies why the gap is not sufficiently closed.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise élimine l'écart de salaires entre les hommes et les femmes, le réduit ou justifie les raisons*

Impact Area : Fair Work (Travail équitable). Pages PDF 330-333. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**FW2** : The company implements fair wage practices.

**FW2.6** : The company maintains a closed gender wage gap, reduces the gap, or justifies why the gap is not sufficiently closed.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.6.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company either:

- a) has reduced the gap by at least 50%
- b) can justify why it did not reduce the gap by 50%.

### 2.6.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company records what actions it took to reduce the wage gap.

### 2.6.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

If it had no gender wage gap, the company maintains a closed gap.

## Intent

To eliminate any wage disparity between men and women in the company.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.6.1] This sub-requirement aligns with FW2.4 and only applies if the company has 250 or more employees in at least one country (based on headcount).

[2.6.1 a; 2.6.3] The gender wage gap is considered closed if it is within a margin of 5%. In other words, the gap is closed if the average pay for women in the company is at least 95% of the average pay for men.

[2.6.1 a; 2.6.3] The gender wage gap is considered closed if the company has a “negative gap”, meaning women are paid more than men on average.

[2.6.1 a] Reducing the gap by at least 50% means to at least halve the gap. For Year 3, this means halving the gap since Year 0. For Year 5, this means halving the gap since Year 3.

[2.6.1 b] The company’s justification, if it has not met the target, demonstrates factors beyond its control.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
