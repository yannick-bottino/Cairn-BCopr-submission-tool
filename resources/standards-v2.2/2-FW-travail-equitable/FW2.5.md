# FW2.5 (TE 2.5) : The company publicly shares its gender wage gap or gaps.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise publie son ou ses écarts de salaires entre les hommes et les femmes*

Impact Area : Fair Work (Travail équitable). Pages PDF 326-329. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**FW2** : The company implements fair wage practices.

**FW2.5** : The company publicly shares its gender wage gap or gaps.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.5.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company publicly shares:

- a) its annual gender wage gap
- b) the gap for each country with at least 250 employees (based on headcount).
- c) the methodology it used to calculate its gender wage gap.

## Intent

To increase public accountability for paying men and women equally.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.5.1] This sub-requirement aligns with FW2.4 and only applies if the company has 250 or more employees in at least one country (based on headcount).

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The reporting scope covers the company. If the company references a corporate group impact report that includes out-of-scope entities, either:
  - the performance of the company is clearly identifiable within the group report
  - the company publicly shares its own impact report on its webpage.
