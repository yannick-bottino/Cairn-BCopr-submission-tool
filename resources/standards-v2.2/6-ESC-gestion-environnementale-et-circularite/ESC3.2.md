# ESC3.2 (GEC 3.2) : The company reduces its use of virgin non-renewable materials.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise réduit son utilisation de matériaux vierges non renouvelables*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 1014-1018. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**ESC3** : The company implements circularity principles to reduce waste and its impact on natural resources.

**ESC3.2** : The company reduces its use of virgin non-renewable materials.

## Compliance criteria

### 3.2.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company’s material inflow indicators confirm that, over the previous 36 months, the company has:

- a) reduced its total use of virgin non-renewable materials
- b) increased the percentage of renewable materials from sustainable sources, or reused or recycled input materials in its products and packaging.

## Intent

To ensure the company reduces its reliance on virgin non-renewable materials, and to avoid the unsustainable depletion of natural resources.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[3.2.1] This sub-requirement applies to products the company produces or has produced on its behalf. Products the company resells may be exempted (e.g. in the case of wholesale/retail). For companies in the Service with Significant Footprint and Agriculture/Growers sectors, this sub-requirement only applies if the company sells a physical product and has control over their production.

[3.2.1] The company automatically meets this sub-requirement if the amount of recycled or renewable materials from sustainable sources it uses, as a percentage of total input materials, meets the values achieved by industry leaders for the product type.

[3.2.1] If legal barriers prevent the company from reducing its use of virgin non-renewable materials in its product, or in its primary or secondary packaging, the company is only required to meet this sub-requirement to the extent legally permissible.

[3.2.1 a] “Virgin non-renewable” materials are finite materials that have not yet been used in the economy, and do not renew in short time periods (adapted from Ellen McArthur Foundation [EN] and European Sustainability Reporting Standards, 2023 [EN]). Examples include iron ore mined from the ground, and plastics made without recycled content.

[3.2.1 a] The company may calculate the amount of virgin non-renewable materials using the inputs from ESC3.1.

[3.2.1 a] The company may reduce its use of virgin non-renewable materials in its products, packaging, or both.

[3.2.1 a] The company may calculate the reduction in its total use of virgin non-renewable materials as an average over the last 36 months.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
