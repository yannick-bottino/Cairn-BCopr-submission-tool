# ESC5.7 (GEC 5.7) : The company works with suppliers to address environmental impacts related to high-risk raw materials.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise collabore avec ses fournisseurs pour remédier aux impacts environnementaux liés aux matières premières à haut risque*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 1141-1148. Échéance : Year 5.

## Requirement

**ESC5** : The company works with suppliers to achieve its environmental objectives.

**ESC5.7** : The company works with suppliers to address environmental impacts related to high-risk raw materials.

## Compliance criteria

### 5.7.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 5 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company works with its prioritized suppliers to prevent and mitigate environmental impacts related to high-risk raw materials, in line with its plan (ESC5.4) and its actions (ESC5.6).

### 5.7.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 5 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The engagement process includes:

- a) an action plan and targets
- b) monitoring progress at least annually
- c) recording specific prevention and mitigation actions and their outcomes.

## Intent

To ensure the company makes progress toward mapping its supply chain and addressing the actual and potential negative environmental impacts of its high-risk raw materials.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[5.7.1] A “high-risk raw material” is any relevant input material that is known or suspected to cause severe negative environmental impacts (as identified under ESC1.7).

[5.7.2] If the supplier is unwilling or unable to take meaningful prevention or mitigation action, the company considers:

- engaging in advocacy or other collective action to address systemic issues and enable the prevention or mitigation action [link to GACA2 The company works collaboratively to advance collective social or environmental impacts]
- choosing to responsibly exit its relationship with the supplier as a last resort.

[5.7.2] Simply integrating environmental criteria into a supplier code of conduct is not a sufficient example of action under this sub-requirement.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
