# ESC1.5 (GEC 1.5) : The company identifies its facilities in or near ecologically sensitive areas, and whether they are negatively affecting these areas.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise identifie ses infrastructures situées en zone écologiquement sensible ou à proximité, et détermine si elles ont une incidence négative*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 920-924. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**ESC1** : The company knows its actual and potential impacts on the environment.

**ESC1.5** : The company identifies its facilities in or near ecologically sensitive areas, and whether they are negatively affecting these areas.

## Compliance criteria

### 1.5.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company assesses its facilities to understand if any are negatively affecting ecologically sensitive areas they are in or near, and records:

- a) the location and size (in hectares) of the facilities
- b) their impacts on biodiversity prioritized by severity and likelihood.

### 1.5.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The assessment includes all of its manufacturing and production facilities.

### 1.5.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company has conducted its assessment to understand if its facilities are in or near ecologically sensitive areas within the past 36 months.

## Intent

To ensure the company develops an understanding of the material environmental impacts related to its direct operations.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[1.5.1] A facility is considered to be “in an ecologically sensitive area” when it is completely or partially located in the ecologically sensitive area.

[1.5.1] A facility is considered to be “near an ecologically sensitive area” when the ecologically sensitive area falls either:

- within the area affected or potentially affected by the company’s operations (sometimes referred to as the area of influence)
- within the radius set by the company (with the facility at its center).

[1.5.1] The company may carry out its assessment in any of the following ways:

- An independent third party carries out the assessment using credible tools or national and regional data available from local authorities or non-governmental organizations.
- The company conducts an assessment designed with credible tools. The tools were developed by reputable organizations in a multi-stakeholder process.
- The company conducts an assessment using the tools in the Implementation Resources.
- The company relies on national and regional data available from local authorities or non-governmental organizations.

[1.5.1] For actual negative impacts, materiality is based on the severity of the impact, while for potential negative impacts it is based on the severity and likelihood of the impact.

[1.5.2] This sub-requirement applies to all facilities the company owns, leases, or manages. The assessment covers all manufacturing and production facilities (including farms). It may exclude offices not involved in manufacturing or production.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The assessment covers the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate assessment, it demonstrates:
  - how the assessment considered its stakeholders and operational impacts,
  - how the assessment outcome is relevant to its context.
