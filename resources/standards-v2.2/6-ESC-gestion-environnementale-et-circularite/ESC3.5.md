# ESC3.5 (GEC 3.5) : The company takes steps to increase the recovery of its products and packaging after their end-of-life.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise prend des mesures pour augmenter la revalorisation de ses produits et emballages après leur fin de vie*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 1060-1064. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**ESC3** : The company implements circularity principles to reduce waste and its impact on natural resources.

**ESC3.5** : The company takes steps to increase the recovery of its products and packaging after their end-of-life.

## Compliance criteria

### 3.5.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company records its actions or programs to improve the infrastructure for the intended recovery option of its material products or packaging.

### 3.5.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (marquage PDF : « Before Year 3 »)*

Before Year 3, the company has introduced these actions or programs in the two countries that represent the largest end-user sales of its material products or packaging.

### 3.5.4

*Échéance du critère : Year 5 (marquage PDF : « For Year 5 »)*

For Year 5, the company either:

- a) increases the scale of existing programs
- b) introduces additional actions or programs.

## Intent

For the company, using its analysis from ESC3.4, to focus its efforts on:

- improving the recovery infrastructure for its products and packaging
- preventing its products and packaging from entering landfills.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[3.5.1; 3.5.2; 3.5.3] This sub-requirement applies to products the company produces or has produced on its behalf. Products the company resells may be exempted (e.g. in the case of wholesale/retail). For companies in the Service with Significant Footprint and Agriculture/Growers sectors, this sub-requirement only applies if the company sells a physical product and has control over their production.

[3.5.1; 3.5.2; 3.5.3] If the product is a fast moving consumer good, this sub-requirement applies to its packaging. Fast moving consumer goods include food products, beverages, toiletries, cleaning supplies, medicines, and other products.

[3.5.1] The “intended recovery option” for each product and packaging refers to the circularity principle from ESC3.3a–d used in its design to reduce waste.

[3.5.2, 3.5.3] The company bases its actions or programs on the findings of its analysis under ESC3.4.

[3.5.2, 3.5.3]  The company may engage in collective action to improve the available recovery infrastructure [Link to GACA2].

[3.5.2, 3.5.3]  Company contributions towards Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) schemes can be considered actions to improve the available infrastructure for recovery, as long as they are:

- linked to the company’s material products and packaging
- contributed in countries with recovery infrastructure barriers.

[3.5.2] The company’s “material products and packaging” are determined as a combination of those that (1) have the highest environmental impacts, as assessed in ESC1.7, and (2) the aggregate volume of products sold.

[3.5.3b] The company may introduce additional actions or programs in either:

- the same countries as under 3.5.3
- other countries with recovery infrastructure barriers, based on its analysis under ESC3.4.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
