# ESC3.1 (GEC 3.1) : The company monitors its material inflow.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise surveille ses approvisionnements en matériaux*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 1007-1013. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**ESC3** : The company implements circularity principles to reduce waste and its impact on natural resources.

**ESC3.1** : The company monitors its material inflow.

## Compliance criteria

### 3.1.1

The company has an overview of its material inflow indicators (see Compliance Criteria 3.1.2 & 3.1.3), separated by products and packaging, for:

- a) the fiscal year before Year 0
- b) each subsequent year.

### 3.1.2

The company’s overview includes the following information about its products (in tonnes).

- a) The total weight of input materials used to manufacture the company’s products (excluding packaging)
- b) The amount of renewable input materials from third-party verified, sustainable sources used to manufacture the company’s products (excluding packaging), by weight and as a percentage of total input materials
- c) The amount of reused or recycled products and materials (non-virgin) used to manufacture the company’s products (excluding packaging), by weight and as a percentage of total input materials

### 3.1.3

The company’s overview includes the following information about its packaging (in tonnes).

- a) The total weight of input materials used to manufacture packaging for the company’s products
- b) The amount of renewable input materials from third-party verified, sustainable sources used to manufacture packaging for the company’s products, by weight and as a percentage of total input materials used to manufacture packaging for the company’s products
- c) The amount of reused or recycled products and materials (non-virgin) used to manufacture packaging for the company’s products, by weight and as a percentage of total input materials used to manufacture packaging for the company’s products

## Intent

For the company to understand its dependence on natural resources, and its impacts on their sustainability. Using renewable materials from sustainable sources gives the company an opportunity to reduce its impact on natural resources.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[3.1.1; 3.1.2; 3.1.3] This sub-requirement applies to products the company produces or has produced on its behalf. Products the company resells may be exempted (e.g. in the case of wholesale/retail). For companies in the Service with Significant Footprint and Agriculture/Growers sectors, this sub-requirement only applies if the company sells a physical product and has control over their production.

[3.1.1; 3.1.2; 3.1.3] The company includes the following material types in its overview.

- Raw materials — natural resources converted into products or services, such as ores, minerals, and wood. Raw materials excludes water, which is captured under ESC1.3.
- Associated process materials — materials that are used in the manufacturing process but not part of the final product, such as lubricants and coolants.
- Semi-manufactured goods or parts — all forms of materials and components other than raw materials that are part of the final product.
- Packaging materials — including paper, cardboard, and plastics.

[3.1.1; 3.1.2; 3.1.3] If the company faces challenges calculating the weight of its raw materials or components, it may use estimates at its first step. However, by Year 3, the company has the actual weight of its raw materials or components.

[3.1.2 b; 3.1.3 b] If the company sources renewable input materials from sustainable sources, it has a third-party assessment or certification confirming the materials were sustainably produced.

[3.1.2 b; 3.1.3 b] The company may evaluate “renewable inputs from sustainable sources” using criteria such as the following (adapted from “responsibly sourced renewable content” in the Cradle to Cradle Certified® Product Standard, Version 4.1 [EN]).

- Compliance with all applicable laws and regulations in the country where farming or harvesting happens
- Operations that respect land rights and land use rights, and are unlikely to displace food production
- Planning, monitoring, management, and continuous impact assessment of farming and harvesting practices
- Maintenance, conservation, or enhancement of biodiversity in the forest and vegetation, or other ecosystems
- Maintenance or enhancement of the productive function of the forest and vegetation or other ecosystem area, and efficient use of harvested materials (e.g. the rate of harvest does not exceed the long-term rate of regrowth)
- Maintenance or enhancement of the health and vitality of the forest and vegetation, or other ecosystem, and its protective systems like soil and water (e.g. controlling erosion of vulnerable soils and slopes)
- Maintenance or enhancement of the High Conservation Values in the forest, vegetation, or other ecosystems (Note: this overlaps with the previous three bullet points)

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The measurement covers the company. If the company references an out-of-scope measurement, it records:
  - the data and mechanism for measuring its own operations
  - how data is consolidated in the out-of-scope parent’s reporting.
