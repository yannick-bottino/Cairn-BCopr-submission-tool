# ESC1.1 (GEC 1.1) : The company monitors waste production from its operations and where it ends up.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise surveille la production de déchets issus de ses activités et leur destination finale*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 896-903. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**ESC1** : The company knows its actual and potential impacts on the environment.

**ESC1.1** : The company monitors waste production from its operations and where it ends up.

## Compliance criteria

### 1.1.1

The company measures and records its annual hazardous and non-hazardous waste production:

- a) in the last fiscal year
- b) in each subsequent year.

### 1.1.2

The company’s non-hazardous waste is measured in tonnes and recorded as:

- a) the total amount of waste generated
- b) the amount of non-hazardous waste diverted from disposal, and how much is:
  - i) prepared for reuse
  - ii) recycled
  - iii) recovered in another way.
- c) the amount of non-hazardous waste disposed of, including by:
  - i) incineration (without energy recovery)
  - ii) landfilling
  - iii) other disposal methods.

### 1.1.3

The company’s hazardous waste is measured in tonnes and recorded as:

- a) the total amount of hazardous waste generated
- b) the amount of hazardous waste diverted from disposal, and how much is:
  - i) recycled
  - ii) recovered in another way
- c) the amount of hazardous waste disposed of, including by:
  - i) incineration (without energy recovery)
  - ii) landfilling
  - iii) other disposal methods.

### 1.1.4

If the company produces or trades food products it also measures (in tonnes):

- a) the total amount of food waste generated
- b) the amount of food waste diverted from disposal, and how much is:
  - i) re-used for human consumption or animal feed
  - ii) recycled or reused by-products
  - iii) used in energy recovery;
  - iv) recovered in another way
- c) the amount of food waste disposed of, including by:
  - i) incineration (without energy recovery)
  - ii) landfilling
  - iii) other disposal methods.

### 1.1.5

If the company cannot confirm where its waste goes, it assumes the waste goes to landfill.

### 1.1.6

Before Year 0, the company:

- a) tracks and records waste generation for all of its facilities identified as material in accordance with ESC1.7
- b) has a plan to extend the tracking and recording of waste generation to all remaining facilities.

### 1.1.7

For Year 5, the company has completed the tracking and recording of waste generation for all facilities.

## Intent

For the company to measure how much waste it creates and diverts. Monitoring waste enables the company to take actions that follow the waste hierarchy and food waste hierarchy.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[1.1.2, 1.1.3, 1.1.4] The measurement refers to metric tonnes. One metric tonne is 1000 kilograms.

[1.1.2 b, 1.1.3 b, 1.1.4 b] “Waste diversion” means the prevention and reduction of waste through

- reducing waste at its source
- recycling
- reusing
- other recovery methods.

[1.1.2 b, 1.1.3 b, 1.1.4 b] “Waste diversion” aims to avoid sending waste to landfill and other forms of disposal that prevent recovery.

[1.1.2 c.i, 1.1.3 c.i, 1.1.4 c.i] Incineration with (or without) energy recovery is defined as the controlled burning of waste at high temperature. It is with energy recovery when the energy created in the combustion process is harnessed for re-use, for example for power generation. It is without energy recovery when the heat generated by combustion is dissipated in the environment (European Sustainability Reporting Standards - E5) [EN].

[1.1.3] “Hazardous waste” means wastes that because of their chemical reactivity, toxic, explosive, corrosive, radioactive or other characteristics, cause danger, or are likely to cause danger, to health or the environment. (adapted from the European Commission) [EN].

- reducing waste at its source
- recycling
- reusing
- other recovery methods.

[1.1.4] For Agriculture/Growers food waste includes organic waste, excluding what is left on the field.

- reducing waste at its source
- recycling
- reusing
- other recovery methods.

[1.1.6] This sub-requirement applies to facilities the company has operational control over.

[1.1.6] This sub-requirement addresses the company’s own facilities, while waste in their supply chains is captured in ESC5.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The measurement covers the company. If the company references an out-of-scope measurement, it records:
  - the data and mechanism for measuring its own facilities
  - how data is consolidated in the out-of-scope parent’s reporting.
