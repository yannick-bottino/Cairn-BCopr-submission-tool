# ESC4.5 (GEC 4.5) : The company publicly shares the effectiveness of its environmental strategy.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise partage publiquement l'efficacité de sa stratégie environnementale*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 1097-1099. Échéance : Year 5.

## Requirement

**ESC4** : The company takes action to prevent and mitigate its actual and potential negative environmental impacts.

**ESC4.5** : The company publicly shares the effectiveness of its environmental strategy.

## Compliance criteria

### 4.5.1

The company makes a report on its environmental strategies, including its biodiversity and water stewardship strategies, publicly available on its webpage.

### 4.5.2

The company made or updated its report in the last 36 months.

### 4.5.3

The company’s report includes:

- a) progress on targets and indicators
- b) how it evaluated the effectiveness of its strategies.

### 4.5.4

Where the strategies are ineffective, the company’s report sets out:

- a) what lessons it has learned
- b) what it plans to do differently
- c) how it has updated its strategies.

## Intent

To increase public accountability and make it easier for companies to learn from one another.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[4.5.1] Where applicable, this sub-requirement refers to the company’s progress against its:

- overall environmental strategy, under ESC4.2
- biodiversity transition plan, under ESC4.3
- water stewardship strategy, under ESC4.4.

[4.5.1] The company may publish the information in an annual report or a standalone sustainability and impact report [Link to PSG6.2, HR2.5].

[4.5.3 b] The company reports its evaluation of the strategies’ effectiveness every 36 months at minimum (aligned with when the evaluation happens).

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The reporting scope covers the company. If the company references a corporate group report that includes out-of-scope entities, either:
  - the performance of the company is clearly identifiable within the group report
  - the company publicly shares its own report on its webpage.
