# ESC2.1 (GEC 2.1) : The company has a strategy to address its actual and potential negative environmental impacts.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise a mis en place une stratégie pour remédier à ses impacts négatifs réels et potentiels sur l'environnement*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 950-959. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**ESC2** : The company has a strategy to address its actual and potential negative environmental impacts, and to support staying within ecological

**ESC2.1** : The company has a strategy to address its actual and potential negative environmental impacts.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.1.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company’s strategy addresses the material negative environmental impacts in its direct operations and value chain identified in ESC1.7.

### 2.1.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The strategy, or a related implementation plan, includes:

- a) quantified targets for the next 36 months at minimum
- b) indicators
- c) clearly defined roles and responsibilities
- d) a plan to engage and work with stakeholders
- e) how the company prioritizes avoidance and reduction of negative environmental impacts
- f) how the company uses its water and biodiversity assessments (where applicable) and:
  - i) protects ecologically sensitive areas or areas at water risk that its facilities are in or near
  - ii) sets objectives to avoid and reduce negative impacts on those areas
- g) how the company’s business model aligns with this strategy, or how it plans to align it.

### 2.1.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The strategy is approved by the highest governing body or executive team.

### 2.1.4

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The strategy was adopted or reviewed in the last twelve months.

## Intent

For the company to create an environmental strategy informed by its assessment of actual and potential material environmental impacts.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.1.1] The strategy sets out how the company intends to address each of its material environmental issues. More specifically, it describes how the company will:

- assess the potential negative environmental impacts of working with potential clients and projects, and on investments if applicable [link to ESC2.6; ESC2.7]
- use inputs from stakeholders [links to PSG2]
- prevent and mitigate actual and potential negative environmental impacts [links to ESC4]
- evaluate the effectiveness of the strategy and update it as required [link to ESC4.2]
- work with suppliers to achieve environmental objectives and trace raw materials in the supply chain to their origins [link to ESC5].

[2.1.2] The company may include collective action or other forms of collaboration in its strategy [Link to GACA2].

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The strategy is embedded, at minimum, at the highest governance level within the company. If the company references a strategy belonging to an out-of-scope parent, it records evidence of roll-out, enforcement and accountability mechanisms to support the strategy.
- References to the highest governing body or executive team mean those within the company’s scope of certification. This means that approval by an out-of-scope parent body does not meet the sub-requirement, unless the company has additional approval at the highest governing body or executive team within its scope of certification.
