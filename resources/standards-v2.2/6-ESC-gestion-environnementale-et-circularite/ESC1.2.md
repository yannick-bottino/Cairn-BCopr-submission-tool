# ESC1.2 (GEC 1.2) : The company monitors its energy use.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise surveille sa consommation énergétique*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 904-909. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**ESC1** : The company knows its actual and potential impacts on the environment.

**ESC1.2** : The company monitors its energy use.

## Compliance criteria

### 1.2.1

The company measures and records its annual energy use:

- a) in the last fiscal year
- b) in each subsequent year.

### 1.2.2

The company’s energy use is measured by:

- a) total energy in giga-joules, watt-hours or multiples
- b) total renewable energy in giga-joules, watt-hours or multiples
- c) percentage of self-generated renewable energy
- d) percentage of renewable electricity
- e) energy intensity, as either:
  - i) total energy use per $million of revenue
  - ii) total energy use per unit of product or service.

### 1.2.3

Before Year 0, the company:

- a)  tracks and records energy use for all of its facilities identified as material in accordance with ESC1.7
- b) has a plan to extend the tracking and recording of energy use to all remaining facilities.

### 1.2.4

For Year 5, the company completes the tracking and recording of energy use for all facilities.

## Intent

For the company to measure its energy efficiency and track the increase in its use of renewable energy.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[1.2.1] The company’s energy use (including renewable energy) includes fuel use and its consumption of purchased or acquired:

- electricity
- heat
- steam
- cooling.

[1.2.2c] “Self-generated renewable energy” means energy that is generated on-site by or for the company.

[1.2.3] This sub-requirement applies to facilities the company has operational control over.

[1.2.3] This sub-requirement addresses the company’s own facilities, while energy use in their supply chains is captured in ESC5.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

The measurement covers the company. If the company references an out-of-scope measurement, it records:

- the data and mechanism for measuring its own facilities
- how data is consolidated in the out-of-scope parent’s reporting.
