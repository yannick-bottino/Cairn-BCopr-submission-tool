# ESC4.1 (GEC 4.1) : The company implements actions to prevent or mitigate its actual and potential negative environmental impacts.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise met en place des actions pour prévenir ou atténuer ses impacts environnementaux négatifs réels et potentiels*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 1065-1076. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**ESC4** : The company takes action to prevent and mitigate its actual and potential negative environmental impacts.

**ESC4.1** : The company implements actions to prevent or mitigate its actual and potential negative environmental impacts.

## Compliance criteria

### 4.1.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company takes action to prevent or mitigate its actual and potential negative environmental impacts:

- a) in the twelve months before Year 3
- b) in each subsequent year.

### 4.1.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company records the action and any demonstrable output.

### 4.1.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The action is related to the company’s operations or value chain.

## Intent

To ensure the company’s awareness of potential negative environmental impacts leads to prevention and mitigation actions. Prevention and mitigation is proactive, meaning it addresses a potential impact before it becomes an actual impact.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[4.1.1; 4.1.2; 4.1.3] The company automatically meets this sub-requirement if it improved its environmental impact, as measured under the following (where applicable):

[4.1.2] The company’s action has a “demonstrable output” (if not yet a measurable positive outcome). Demonstrable outputs may include the company implementing the following, among other actions:

- Biodiversity outputs
  - Practices that help to maintain or enhance biodiversity, carbon storage or sequestration, soil health, or the health of other ecosystems, and increase the stock of native species
- Water outputs
  - Measures to optimize water use
  - Measures to improve water quality
  - Ways to reuse and recover water (or other circular water management approaches)
- Waste outputs
  - Actions that follow the waste hierarchy and food waste hierarchy (e.g. reducing waste or increasing waste recovery)
- Energy outputs
  - Measures to improve energy efficiency
  - Moving to renewable electricity or energy
- Pollution outputs
  - Measures to minimize pollution (of soil, water, and air)
  - Measures to identify and reduce potential hazardous substances and substances of concern in products and packaging
- Animal welfare outputs
  - Measures to improve animal welfare conditions in line with the five freedoms of animal welfare

[4.1.2] The intent is only for the company to take action, not necessarily to achieve positive outcomes.  This acknowledges that measurable reductions in negative impacts may take longer time to manifest or measure. In any case, the company records impact data or metrics to measure the outputs and any positive outcomes of its actions.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
