# ESC1.4 (GEC 1.4) : The company identifies its facilities in areas at water risk and monitors their water consumption or withdrawal.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise identifie ses infrastructures dans les zones à risque hydrique et surveille leur consommation ou leur prélèvement d'eau*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 915-919. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**ESC1** : The company knows its actual and potential impacts on the environment.

**ESC1.4** : The company identifies its facilities in areas at water risk and monitors their water consumption or withdrawal.

## Compliance criteria

### 1.4.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company identifies any of its facilities in areas at physical water risk.

### 1.4.2

*Échéance du critère : Before Year 0 (marquage PDF : « Before Year 0 »)*

Before Year 0, the company:

- a) tracks and records water consumption or withdrawal for any of its facilities in areas at physical water risk identified as material in accordance with ESC1.7
- b) has a plan to extend the tracking and recording of water consumption or withdrawal to all remaining facilities in areas at physical water risk.

### 1.4.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 5 (marquage PDF : « For Year 5 »)*

For Year 5, the company has completed the tracking and recording of water consumption or withdrawal for all facilities in areas of physical water risk.

### 1.4.4

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company has measured and recorded its annual water consumption or withdrawal in cubic meters (m3) in areas at physical water risk:

- a) in the last fiscal year
- b) in each subsequent year.

## Intent

To ensure the company develops an understanding of the material environmental impacts related to its direct operations.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[1.4.1] This sub-requirement applies to facilities the company has operational control over.

[1.4.1] The company considers different types of physical water risk to assess which of its facilities are relevant to this sub-requirement.

[1.4.1] To identify if a facility is in an area at water risk, the company may, for example:

- contact the local authority that controls (or has an overview of) the watershed to understand the pressure on it, combined with an analysis of water risk.
- use any of the tools in the Implementation Resources.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The assessment covers the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate assessment, it demonstrates:
  - how the assessment considered its stakeholders and operational impacts
  - how the assessment outcome is relevant to its context.
