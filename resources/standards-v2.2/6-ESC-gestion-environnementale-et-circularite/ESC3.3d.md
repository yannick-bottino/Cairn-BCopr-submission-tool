# ESC3.3d (GEC 3.3d) : The company’s products are able to recirculate at their end-of-life

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 1049-1055. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**ESC3** : The company implements circularity principles to reduce waste and its impact on natural resources.

**ESC3.3d** : The company’s products are able to recirculate at their end-of-life

## Compliance criteria

### 3.3d.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company records:

- a) the amount of its products, as a percentage of the total weight of its products (excluding packaging) that were designed for:
  - i) recycling
  - ii) recirculation by the biological cycle
- b) the amount of packaging, as a percentage of the total weight of its packaging, that was designed for:
  - i) recycling
  - ii) recirculation by the biological cycle.

### 3.3d.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company’s products or packaging have been designed for recycling or recirculation by the biological cycle in a way that exceeds standard practice.

## Intent

For the company to enable recirculation of its products and materials at their end of life, by designing for recycling and recirculation by the biological cycle.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[3.3d.1; 3.3d.2] This sub-requirement applies to products the company produces or has produced on its behalf. Products the company resells may be exempted (e.g. in the case of wholesale/retail). For companies in the Service with Significant Footprint and Agriculture/Growers sectors, this sub-requirement only applies if the company sells a physical product and has control over their production.

[3.3d.1; 3.3d.2] If the product is a fast moving consumer good, this sub-requirement applies to its packaging. Fast moving consumer goods include food products, beverages, toiletries, cleaning supplies, medicines, and other products.

[3.3d.1; 3.3d.2] The company follows the hierarchy of recovery cycles, and considers the available infrastructure in locations where its products are sold.

[3.3d.1] If the company has multiple product lines, it calculates the percentage indicators across its entire portfolio to establish the baseline.

[3.3d.1a.i; 3.3d.1b.i] When evaluating its products and packaging that are “designed for recycling”, the company considers whether they are compatible with existing recycling systems that currently operate at scale.

[3.3d.1a.ii; 3.3d.1b.ii]  Recirculation by the biological cycle includes, for example:

- composting;
- anaerobic digestion.
- composting;
- anaerobic digestion.

[3.3d.2] The company explains how it defines “standard practice”, and how its design exceeds it. The explanation can use quantitative or qualitative measures.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The measurement covers the company. If the company references an out-of-scope measurement, it records:
  - the data and mechanism for measuring its own operations
  - how data is consolidated in the out-of-scope parent’s reporting.
