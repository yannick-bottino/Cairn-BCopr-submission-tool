# ESC5.3 (GEC 5.3) : The company works with suppliers to prevent or mitigate their most material environmental impacts.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise collabore avec ses fournisseurs pour prévenir ou atténuer leurs impacts environnementaux les plus matériels*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 1116-1123. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**ESC5** : The company works with suppliers to achieve its environmental objectives.

**ESC5.3** : The company works with suppliers to prevent or mitigate their most material environmental impacts.

## Compliance criteria

### 5.3.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company:

- a) identifies prioritized suppliers related to its most material environmental impacts (ESC1.7)
- b) records the criteria it used to prioritize suppliers.

### 5.3.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company works with its prioritized suppliers to prevent or mitigate their most material environmental impacts by

- a) agreeing on targets with each prioritized supplier
- b) monitoring progress at least annually

### 5.3.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

For Years 3 and 5, the company records specific prevention and mitigation actions and their outcomes.

## Intent

To ensure the company’s awareness of potential negative impacts in the supply chain leads to prevention and mitigation actions.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[5.3.1] This sub-requirement applies to suppliers at all tiers.

[5.3.1] The company’s actions are linked to the material impact. Material environmental impacts are determined under ESC1.7. For example, if a supplier operates in an area at water risk, the company works with the supplier to reduce the amount of water it uses.

[5.3.1] The most material environmental impact may include animal welfare, as well.

[5.3.2] If the supplier is unwilling or unable to take meaningful prevention or mitigation action, the company considers:

- engaging in advocacy or other collective action to address systemic issues and enable the prevention or mitigation action [link to GACA2].
- choosing to responsibly exit its relationship with the supplier as a last resort.

[5.3.2] Simply integrating environmental criteria into a supplier code of conduct is not a sufficient example of action under this sub-requirement.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
