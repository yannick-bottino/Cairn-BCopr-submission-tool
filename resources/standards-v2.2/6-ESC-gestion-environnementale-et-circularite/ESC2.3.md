# ESC2.3 (GEC 2.3) : The company has a water stewardship strategy that limits water use in its operations and value chain to within sustainable thresholds.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise a mis en place une stratégie de gestion de l'eau qui limite la consommation d'eau à des seuils soutenables*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 970-976. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**ESC2** : The company has a strategy to address its actual and potential negative environmental impacts, and to support staying within ecological

**ESC2.3** : The company has a water stewardship strategy that limits water use in its operations and value chain to within sustainable thresholds.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.3.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

If the company identified water-related impacts as material in its assessment of environmental impacts then the company’s water stewardship strategy includes:

- a) an emphasis on a circular water management approach
- b) quantified targets for its own facilities
- c) a plan to engage its material suppliers (by impact) to understand:
  - i) what actions they take to manage their water-related impact
  - ii) whether they are located in areas at water risk
- d) actions to reduce water use and minimize water pollution, especially in areas at water risk
- e) how the company integrates Indigenous Peoples’ local knowledge or traditional ecological knowledge and nature-based solutions.

### 2.3.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company identifies if contextual data exists for the watershed or basin in which the company has its facilities. If data is available, the company's strategy:

- a) describes how it ensures to stay within its proportionate allocation of local renewable water supplies.

### 2.3.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The strategy is approved by the highest governing body or executive team.

### 2.3.4

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The strategy was adopted or reviewed in the last twelve months.

## Intent

For companies to:

- address their water-related impacts and contribute to the responsible, sustainable management of freshwater resources
- go beyond water efficiency practices and lead collective action in the watersheds of their value chain.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.3.1; 2.3.2.; 2.3.3; 2.3.4] This sub-requirement only applies if the company identifies water-related impacts as material in its assessment of environmental impacts [link to ESC1.7].

[2.3.1 a] Implementing a circular water management approach may help the company reduce its water-related environmental impact to within ecological thresholds.

[2.3.1 b; 2.3.2] The company’s water stewardship strategy is expected to set quantitative targets, as signaled under ESC2.1. Before Year 3, the company does both of the following.

- Sets targets for its own operations (facilities).
- Has an action plan to engage its value chain to gather data and measure progress over time. The company may prioritize its  material suppliers (by impact) and other material members of its value chain (by impact). These foundational steps enable the company to set concrete targets for its value chain in the future.

[2.3.1 c] The company's "material suppliers" are those it identifies as having the most actual and potential negative environmental impacts.

[2.3.1 c] Actions the company’s suppliers take to manage their water-related impacts may relate to water quantity or quality (with a preference for both).

[2.3.1 e] Whenever relevant and possible, the company co-creates with Indigenous Peoples, incorporating their local or traditional knowledge into the plan, and building on their good stewardship and nature-based solutions.

[2.3.1 e] The company compensates Indigenous Peoples appropriately when integrating their views or knowledge, and acknowledges their time and efforts.

[2.3.2] The company’s water stewardship plan prioritizes its facilities located in regions with physical water risk, as identified in ESC1.4.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The strategy is embedded, at minimum, at the highest governance level within the company. If the company references a strategy belonging to an out-of-scope parent, it records evidence of roll-out, enforcement and accountability mechanisms to support the strategy.
- References to the highest governing body or executive team mean those within the company’s scope of certification. This means that approval by an out-of-scope parent body does not meet the sub-requirement, unless the company has additional approval at the highest governing body or executive team within its scope of certification.
