# ESC2.4 (GEC 2.4) : The company’s policies and procedures address its material environmental impact areas.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : Les politiques et procédures de l'entreprise traitent les enjeux d'impacts environnementaux matériels*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 977-981. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**ESC2** : The company has a strategy to address its actual and potential negative environmental impacts, and to support staying within ecological thresholds.

**ESC2.4** : The company’s policies and procedures address its material environmental impact areas.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.4.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company explicitly addresses each material environmental impact area with a policy or procedure.

### 2.4.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The scope of each policy and procedure matches the nature of the material environmental impact area.

## Intent

To ensure the company’s policies and procedures address all material environmental impact areas.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.4.1; 2.4.2] The company determines its material environmental impacts under ESC1.7.

[2.4.1; 2.4.2] The company uses policies and procedures to prevent or mitigate its potential negative environmental impacts.

[2.4.2] Matching the “scope” of policies and procedures to material environmental impact areas means ensuring they apply where the impacts are relevant (e.g. for the company’s own operations or the supply chain). For example, if waste management is a material impact in the company’s supply chain, a policy to address waste management in the company’s own operations would not be sufficient, because the scope is different.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The policies and procedures are embedded, at minimum, at the highest level within the company. If the company references policies and procedures belonging to an out-of-scope parent, it records evidence of roll-out, enforcement and accountability mechanisms to support the policies and procedures.
