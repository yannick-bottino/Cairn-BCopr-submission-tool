# ESC5.6 (GEC 5.6) : The company traces more high-risk raw materials to their origin.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise trace une part croissante de ses matières premières à haut risque*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 1135-1140. Échéance : Year 5.

## Requirement

**ESC5** : The company works with suppliers to achieve its environmental objectives.

**ESC5.6** : The company traces more high-risk raw materials to their origin.

## Compliance criteria

### 5.6.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 5 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company increases the proportion of high-risk raw materials that it can trace to their origin, in line with its plan under ESC5.4.

## Intent

To ensure the company makes progress toward mapping the high-risk raw materials in its supply chain.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[5.6.1] The “origin” means the location where the commodity or raw material was produced or harvested, including if it was in a high-risk country.

A “high-risk country” is a country with a high rate of deforestation and at higher risk of deforestation.

[5.6.1] A “high-risk raw material” is any relevant input material that is known or suspected to cause severe negative environmental impacts (as identified under ESC1.7).

[5.6.1] If the company knows the origin of its high-risk raw materials and has mitigated their environmental impacts, it continues to improve its ability to trace raw-materials and mitigate impacts in its supply chain.

[5.6.1] The company calculates the proportion of its high-risk raw materials that it can trace to their known origin using the following formula.

- Volume of high-risk raw materials with known origins / total volume of high-risk raw materials sourced.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
