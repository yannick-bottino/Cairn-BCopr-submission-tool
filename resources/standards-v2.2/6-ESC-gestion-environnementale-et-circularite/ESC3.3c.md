# ESC3.3c (GEC 3.3c) : The company’s products are able to recirculate after use.

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 1042-1048. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**ESC3** : The company implements circularity principles to reduce waste and its impact on natural resources.

**ESC3.3c** : The company’s products are able to recirculate after use.

## Compliance criteria

### 3.3c.1

The company records:

- a) the amount of its products, as a percentage of the total weight of its products (excluding packaging) that were designed for:
  - i) disassembly
  - ii) remanufacturing or refurbishment
- b) the amount of its packaging, as a percentage of the total weight its packaging, that was designed for:
  - i) disassembly
  - ii) remanufacturing or refurbishment.

## Intent

For the company to enable recirculation of its products and materials after they are used, by designing for disassembly, remanufacturing, and refurbishment.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[3.3c.1] This sub-requirement applies to products the company produces or has produced on its behalf. Products the company resells may be exempted (e.g. in the case of wholesale/retail). For companies in the Service with Significant Footprint and Agriculture/Growers sectors, this sub-requirement only applies if the company sells a physical product and has control over their production.

[3.3c.1] If the product is a fast moving consumer good, this sub-requirement applies to its packaging. Fast moving consumer goods include food products, beverages, toiletries, cleaning supplies, medicines, and other products.

[3.3c.1] If a product or packaging meets multiple criteria (e.g. it is designed for both disassembly and refurbishment), the company counts it only once in its calculations.

[3.3c.1] If the company designs its products for disassembly, it has information on how their designs improve the ease of disassembly. The company may evaluate “ease of disassembly” using criteria such as the following (adapted from Cradle to Cradle Certified® Product Standard, Version 4.1 [EN]).

- The product has at least one design feature that improves its ease of disassembly compared to an alternative product.
- The design avoids processes that involve the loss of specific materials in the product to recover other materials (e.g. burning plastics to recover metals).
- Disassembly instructions are publicly available, and third parties can follow a standardized disassembly process, including the general public using common tools (e.g. hammers, screwdrivers, or pliers).

[3.3c.1] A product is "designed for remanufacturing" if it has the following design elements (adapted from Cradle to Cradle Certified® Product Standard, Version 4.1 [EN]).

- The product includes at least one design feature that lets users:
  - Disassemble it
  - Remove functioning, reusable parts
  - Use those parts to be rebuilt into a new product.
- Products rebuilt using old product parts can perform at the same level as a newly manufactured product (or better).

[3.3c.1] A product is "designed for refurbishment" if it has the following design elements (adapted from Cradle to Cradle Certified® Product Standard, Version 4.1 [EN]).

- The product includes at least one design feature that lets users return a product to good working condition, without changing its functionality, by
  - replacing or repairing major components that are faulty or close to failure
  - making cosmetic changes to update its appearance, such as cleaning, changing fabric, painting, or refinishing
  - updating its specifications.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The measurement covers the company. If the company references an out-of-scope measurement, it records:
  - the data and mechanism for measuring its own operations
  - how data is consolidated in the out-of-scope parent’s reporting.
