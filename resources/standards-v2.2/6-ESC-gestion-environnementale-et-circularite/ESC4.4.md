# ESC4.4 (GEC 4.4) : The company makes progress on its water stewardship strategy and evaluates its effectiveness.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise progresse dans sa stratégie de gestion de l'eau et évalue son efficacité*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 1092-1096. Échéance : Year 5.

## Requirement

**ESC4** : The company takes action to prevent and mitigate its actual and potential negative environmental impacts.

**ESC4.4** : The company makes progress on its water stewardship strategy and evaluates its effectiveness.

## Compliance criteria

### 4.4.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 5 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company makes progress against its targets and indicators (measured at an interval of no more than 36 months), in line with its water stewardship strategy defined under ESC2.3.

### 4.4.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 5 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company evaluates the effectiveness of its water stewardship strategy.

### 4.4.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 5 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The evaluation results are shared with the highest governing body or executive team.

### 4.4.4

*Échéance du critère : Year 5 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

Where the strategy is ineffective, the company records:

- a) what lessons it has learned
- b) what it plans to do differently
- c) how it has updated the strategy.

### 4.4.5

*Échéance du critère : Year 5 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company updates its water stewardship strategy to add targets and indicators for its value chain.

## Intent

For the company to put its water stewardship strategy into practice and ensure it works as intended. Water stewardship is an area of constant development, and companies can better understand the outcome of their interventions over time. The intent of the strategy is to evaluate, learn, and continuously improve.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[4.4.1; 4.4.2; 4.4.3; 4.4.4; 4.4.5] This sub-requirement only applies if the company identifies water-related impacts as material in its assessment of environmental impacts [link to ESC1.7].

[4.4.1; 4.4.5] Because the company is only required to set targets for its value chain by Year 3, it is not expected to make progress against those targets or evaluate their effectiveness until after Year 3.

[4.4.1] The “water stewardship strategy” refers to the output of ESC2.3, which sets baselines for improvement.

[4.4.1] Companies are primarily accountable to the internal targets and indicators in their water stewardship strategy — they are not expected to single-handedly improve the state of a watershed that may have many other users.

[4.4.2] Evaluating the effectiveness of the company’s strategy goes beyond assessing its targets and indicators. The company considers other sources of information — such as expert advice, or emerging guidance on a particular environmental area — to understand if its strategy still sets the right approach to manage its material environmental impacts.

[4.4.4 c] The company updates its strategy as new information emerges (for example on its potential environmental impact).

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
- References to the highest governing body or executive team mean those within the company’s scope of certification. This means that sharing the results with an out-of-scope parent body does not meet the sub-requirement, unless the company has also shared the results with the highest governing body or executive team within its scope of certification.
