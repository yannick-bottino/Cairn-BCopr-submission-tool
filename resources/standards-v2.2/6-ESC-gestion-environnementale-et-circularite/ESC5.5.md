# ESC5.5 (GEC 5.5) : The company sources deforestation-free raw materials.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise s'approvisionne en matières premières non issues de la déforestation*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 1130-1134. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**ESC5** : The company works with suppliers to achieve its environmental objectives.

**ESC5.5** : The company sources deforestation-free raw materials.

## Compliance criteria

### 5.5.1

If the company sources raw materials from a high-risk country, it has evidence that they:

- a) are deforestation-free
- b) were produced in accordance with the relevant legislation of the country of production.

### 5.5.2

The company evaluates the following priority raw materials potentially linked to deforestation at minimum.

- a) Cattle
- b) Cocoa
- c) Coffee
- d) Oil palm
- e) Rubber
- f) Soya
- g) Wood

## Intent

For the company to confirm that its supply chain for high-risk raw materials is deforestation-free.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[5.5.1] A “high-risk country” is a country with a high rate of deforestation and at higher risk of deforestation. “High-risk” may also relate to a particular region within a country.

[5.5.1 a] Evidence that raw materials are deforestation-free may include any of the following.

- Geolocation of where raw materials were produced with verifiable information confirming they are deforestation-free
- Sustainability certifications that include provisions to ensure producers meet local legislation, and cover deforestation-free production and sourcing of raw materials. The traceability type must offer geolocation data (i.e. Mass Balance is not accepted). Acceptable certifications may include, for example:
  - RSPO (palm oil)
  - UTZ/Rainforest Alliance (cocoa, coffee)
  - Fairtrade International (cocoa, coffee)
  - ProTerra Standard (soy)
  - FSC certification (timber and other wood products and derivatives)
- Approved lists of low-risk countries for deforestation

[5.5.2] The company may expand its evaluation beyond the priority raw materials in the Compliance Criteria to include other high-risk raw materials, depending on its context.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
