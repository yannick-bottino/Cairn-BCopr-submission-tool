# ESC2.5 (GEC 2.5) : Workers in relevant roles receive the guidance they need to implement the environmental policies and procedures.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : Les collaborateurs exerçant des fonctions pertinentes reçoivent les conseils dont ils ont besoin pour mettre en œuvre les politiques et procédures environnementales*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 982-984. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**ESC2** : The company has a strategy to address its actual and potential negative environmental impacts, and to support staying within ecological thresholds.

**ESC2.5** : Workers in relevant roles receive the guidance they need to implement the environmental policies and procedures.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.5.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

Workers in relevant roles receive training, guidance documents, or tools to help implement the environmental policies and procedures.

### 2.5.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The guidance is tailored for the relevant role or department.

## Intent

To ensure the right people are prepared to implement the environmental policies and procedures.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.5.1; 2.5.2] Relevant roles can be at any level within a company. They may range from members of the highest governing body to workers who work on environmental issues.

[2.5.1] “The environmental policies and procedures” are those related to each material environmental impact area (ESC1.7)

[2.5.1] Workers in relevant roles are given information tailored to their role. Generic training about the company’s material environmental issues is not sufficient.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
