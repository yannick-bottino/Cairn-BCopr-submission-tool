# ESC1.8 (GEC 1.8) : The company publicly shares its material environmental issues.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise partage publiquement ses enjeux environnementaux matériels*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 946-949. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**ESC1** : The company knows its actual and potential impacts on the environment.

**ESC1.8** : The company publicly shares its material environmental issues.

## Compliance criteria

### 1.8.1

The company's material environmental issues (ESC1.7) are available on its webpage or the company has another way for stakeholders to easily access it.

### 1.8.2

The company publicly shares the methodology it used in its assessment of material environmental issues.

## Intent

To increase public accountability and enable companies to compare their material environmental issues.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[1.8.1, 1.8.2] The company's material environmental issues are available in one of the following.

- Its annual report
- An annual sustainability report
- An integrated annual report and sustainability report
- Another public report available on its webpage
- Its annual report
- An annual sustainability report

*(3 paragraphe(s) répété(s) à l'identique dans le PDF, non repris.)*

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The reporting scope covers the company. If the company references a corporate group report that includes out-of-scope entities, either:
  - the performance of the company is clearly identifiable within the group report
  - the company publicly shares its own report on its webpage.
