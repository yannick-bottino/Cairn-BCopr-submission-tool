# ESC4.2 (GEC 4.2) : The company makes progress on its environmental strategy and evaluates its effectiveness.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise progresse dans sa stratégie environnementale et en évalue l'efficacité*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 1077-1086. Échéance : Year 5.

## Requirement

**ESC4** : The company takes action to prevent and mitigate its actual and potential negative environmental impacts.

**ESC4.2** : The company makes progress on its environmental strategy and evaluates its effectiveness.

## Compliance criteria

### 4.2.1

The company makes progress against its targets and indicators (measured at an interval of no more than 36 months), in line with its environmental strategy defined under ESC2.1.

### 4.2.2

The company evaluates the effectiveness of its environmental strategy.

### 4.2.3

The evaluation results are shared with the highest governing body or executive team.

### 4.2.4

Where the strategy is ineffective, the company records:

- a) what lessons it has learned
- b) what it plans to do differently
- c) how it has updated the strategy.

### 4.2.5

The company’s environmental strategy has targets and indicators to prevent or mitigate negative environmental impacts for its value chain.

## Intent

For the company to put its environmental strategy into practice and ensure it works as intended. Environmental stewardship is an area of constant development, and companies can better understand the outcome of their interventions over time. The intent of the environmental strategy is to evaluate, learn, and continuously improve. Completely eliminating all actual and potential negative impacts is impossible and therefore not the intent.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[4.2.1, 4.2.2] If the company has third-party assurance that its operations and value chain respect animal welfare and stay within ecological thresholds, it can apply the method, tool, or data used to help meet this sub-requirement.

[4.2.1; 4.2.4] The company updates its strategy as new information emerges (for example on its potential environmental impact).

[4..1] The “environmental strategy” refers to the output of ESC2.1, which sets baselines for improvement.

[4.2.2] The company records how it prioritized avoiding and reducing negative environmental impacts while progressing its environmental strategy, in line with ESC2.1.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
- References to the highest governing body or executive team mean those within the company’s scope of certification. This means that sharing the results with an out-of-scope parent body does not meet the sub-requirement, unless the company has also shared the results with the highest governing body or executive team within its scope of certification.
