# ESC5.4 (GEC 5.4) : The company has a time-bound plan to trace the origin and potential environmental impacts of its high-risk raw materials.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise dispose d'un plan, défini dans le temps, pour assurer la traçabilité de l'origine de ses matières premières à haut risque ainsi que leurs impacts environnementaux potentiels*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 1124-1129. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**ESC5** : The company works with suppliers to achieve its environmental objectives.

**ESC5.4** : The company has a time-bound plan to trace the origin and potential environmental impacts of its high-risk raw materials.

## Compliance criteria

### 5.4.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company either:

- a) knows the origin and potential environmental impacts of its high-risk raw materials (as determined under ESC1.7)
- b) has a time-bound plan to trace and fully map its supply chain across all supplier tiers and for all high-risk raw materials.

### 5.4.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company documents the proportion of high-risk raw materials that it can trace to their origin.

## Intent

For the company to fully map its supply chain and mitigate the actual and potential negative environmental impacts of its high-risk raw materials.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[5.4.1] If the company knows the origin of its high-risk raw materials and has mitigated their environmental impacts, it continues to improve its ability to trace raw-materials and mitigate impacts in its supply chain.

[5.4.1; 5.4.2] A “high-risk raw material” is any relevant input material that is known or suspected to cause severe negative environmental impacts (as identified under ESC1.7).

[5.4.1 a] The “origin” means the location where the commodity or raw material was produced or harvested, including if it was in a high-risk country or region. A “high-risk country or region” is a country or a region within a country with a high rate of deforestation and at higher risk of deforestation.

[5.4.2] The company calculates the proportion of its high-risk raw materials that it can trace to their known origin using the following formula.

- Volume of high-risk raw materials with known origins / total volume of high-risk raw materials sourced.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations.
