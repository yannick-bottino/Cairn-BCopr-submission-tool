# ESC3.4 (GEC 3.4) : The company understands the recovery infrastructure available where it sells its products.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise comprend les infrastructures de revalorisation disponibles là où elle vend ses produits*

Impact Area : Environmental Stewardship & Circularity (Gestion environnementale et circularité). Pages PDF 1056-1059. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**ESC3** : The company implements circularity principles to reduce waste and its impact on natural resources.

**ESC3.4** : The company understands the recovery infrastructure available where it sells its products.

## Compliance criteria

### 3.4.1

The company conducts and records an analysis of available recovery infrastructure for its products and packaging after their end-of-life.

The analysis:

- a) covers all the company’s material products and packaging
- b) includes 25% of countries where the company’s products are sold at minimum
- c) is based on desk research or stakeholder engagement
- d) identifies the recovery infrastructure challenges for each included country.

### 3.4.2

The analysis produces a document that summarizes the company’s methodology.

### 3.4.3

The analysis was either completed or updated in the last 36 months.

### 3.4.4

For each update, the company increases the extent of coverage of the analysis (measured as a percentage of aggregate production and number of countries) as compared to the previous analysis.

## Intent

To create a foundation for the company to increase the recovery of its products and packaging under ESC3.5. This analysis helps the company identify hotspots for recovery infrastructure challenges, and tailor its actions toward the areas of highest need.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[3.4.1; 3.4.2; 3.4.3; 3.4.4] This sub-requirement applies to products the company produces or has produced on its behalf. Products the company resells may be exempted (e.g. in the case of wholesale/retail). For companies in the Service with Significant Footprint and Agriculture/Growers sectors, this sub-requirement only applies if the company sells a physical product and has control over their production.

[3.4.1; 3.4.2; 3.4.3; 3.4.4] If the product is a fast moving consumer good, this sub-requirement applies to its packaging. Fast moving consumer goods include food products, beverages, toiletries, cleaning supplies, medicines, and other products.

[3.4.1a] The company’s “material products and packaging” are determined as a combination of those that (1) have the highest environmental impacts, as assessed in ESC1.7, and (2) the aggregate volume of products sold.

[3.4.4] The company may determine the pace at which it increases the coverage of the analysis. The ultimate objective is 100% coverage of all countries where the company’s products are sold.

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

  - how the analysis and methodology outcome is relevant to its context.
