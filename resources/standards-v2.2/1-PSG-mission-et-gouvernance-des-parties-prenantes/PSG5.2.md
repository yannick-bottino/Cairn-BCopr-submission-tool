# PSG5.2 (MGPP 5.2) : Requirements to monitor the company's purpose, social and environmental impact, and stakeholder considerations are enshrined in the highest governing body's terms of reference.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : Les exigences de supervision sont définies dans le cadre de référence de la plus haute instance de direction*

Impact Area : Purpose & Stakeholder Governance (Mission et gouvernance des parties prenantes). Pages PDF 250-254. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**PSG5** : The company’s social and environmental impact and stakeholder considerations are monitored by the highest governing body and

**PSG5.2** : Requirements to monitor the company's purpose, social and environmental impact, and stakeholder considerations are enshrined in the highest governing body's terms of reference.

## Compliance criteria

### 5.2.1

The highest governing body’s written terms of reference explicitly specify that the highest governing body is responsible for overseeing:

- a) the company’s public purpose as defined under PSG1.1
- b) the company’s social and environmental impact
- c) the company’s consideration of its impact on stakeholders.

## Intent

To ensure the highest governing body is accountable for advancing the company purpose, improving its social and environmental performance, and overseeing the implementation of stakeholder governance. By enabling highest governing body oversight of these areas, the company puts the B Corp legal requirement into practice.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[5.2.1] “Terms of reference” means a document that establishes the purpose of a highest governing body or committee and details the roles and specific authority that the highest governing body or committee has to oversee a delegated area of responsibility. (adapted from Financial Reporting Council [EN])

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The terms of references are embedded, at minimum, at the highest level within the company. This means that the company may refer to terms of reference belonging to an out-of-scope parent or create its own terms of reference, as long as they apply to the highest governing body within the scope of certification.
