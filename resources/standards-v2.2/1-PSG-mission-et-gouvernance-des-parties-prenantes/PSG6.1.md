# PSG6.1 (MGPP 6.1) : The company reports its social and environmental performance annually and publicly, with approval from the highest governing body.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : Chaque année, l'entreprise rend compte publiquement de ses performances sociales et environnementales*

Impact Area : Purpose & Stakeholder Governance (Mission et gouvernance des parties prenantes). Pages PDF 272-276. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**PSG6** : The company is transparent about its social and environmental performance.

**PSG6.1** : The company reports its social and environmental performance annually and publicly, with approval from the highest governing body.

## Compliance criteria

### 6.1.1

The company’s social and environmental impact report:

- a) includes progress against all relevant measures of the company’s social and environmental performance and a summary of stakeholder engagement processes
- b) is approved by the highest governing body
- c) is published on the company’s webpage and made accessible to all stakeholders.

### 6.1.2

If the company does not produce annual reports, it can issue a comprehensive report at minimum every second year, while providing lighter interim updates on its webpage or through topic-specific reports.

## Intent

To transparently communicate the company’s social and environmental performance. By doing so, the company:

- demonstrates accountability
- shares progress toward its goals
- upholds its commitment to stakeholders
- provides insight into its impact and strategies for improvement.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[6.1.1.a] The report is comprehensive. It addresses the company’s:

- key social and environmental issues, and the actions it has taken
- social and environmental performance metrics
- progress toward targets
- consideration of stakeholder interests.

[6.1.1.a] “Stakeholder engagement” in this sub-requirement refers to gathering input from stakeholders to help define material topics [Link to PSG2.3].

[6.1.1.a] Participating in a third-party reporting initiative, such as the CDP or the Dow Jones Sustainability Index (DJSI), does not meet this sub-requirement. While these initiatives are valuable, they are not focused on broad-based public transparency for all stakeholders.

[6.1.1.c] “Accessible to all stakeholders” means stakeholders can easily navigate to the report from the company’s homepage.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The reporting scope covers the company. If the company references a corporate group impact report that includes out-of-scope entities, either:
  - the performance of the company is clearly identifiable within the group impact report
  - the company publicly shares its own impact report on its webpage.
- References to the highest governing body mean those within the company’s scope of certification. This means that approval by an out-of-scope parent body does not meet the sub-requirement, unless the company has additional approval at the highest governing body or executive team within its scope of certification.
