# PSG2.2 (MGPP 2.2) : The company has a stakeholder governance policy.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise dispose d'une politique de gouvernance des parties prenantes*

Impact Area : Purpose & Stakeholder Governance (Mission et gouvernance des parties prenantes). Pages PDF 176-182. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**PSG2** : The company considers its impacts on stakeholders in decision-making.

**PSG2.2** : The company has a stakeholder governance policy.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.2.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company’s stakeholder governance policy describes:

- a) what it means to be a company with a stakeholder governance model
- b) the criteria for identifying and prioritizing stakeholders, including a comprehensive map of the company's stakeholders
- c) how decision-makers across the company consider stakeholders in decision-making related to (potential) positive and negative impacts
- d) how the company delivers value to stakeholders through considering their interests
- e) the mechanisms to encourage regular stakeholder engagement.

### 2.2.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The following stakeholders are represented with one or more engagement mechanisms.

- a) Workers of the company or its subsidiaries
- b) Suppliers
- c) Customers
- d) Investors
- e) The community where offices or facilities of the company, its subsidiaries, its suppliers, or its investments are located
- f) The local or global environment

### 2.2.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The policy is approved by the highest governing body.

### 2.2.4

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The policy is communicated to company workers and available for them to access.

## Intent

To ensure the company considers the impact of its decision-making on its stakeholders. By doing so, the company puts the B Corp legal requirement into practice.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.2.1.b] The criteria for identifying and prioritizing stakeholders includes a comprehensive map of the company’s stakeholders.

[2.2.2] The company can use different stakeholder engagement mechanisms flexibly, as long as all the stakeholders in the Compliance Criteria are covered.

[2.2.3] The company may cover elements of the policy in different policies and procedures for decision-makers (i.e. not in a single stakeholder governance document) — as long as they are approved by the highest governing body.

[2.2.4] The company explains how it communicates the stakeholder governance policy to its workers (e.g. with training).

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- References to the highest governing body mean those within the company’s scope of certification. This means that approval by an out-of-scope parent body does not meet the sub-requirement, unless the company has additional approval at the highest governing body within its scope of certification.
- The policy is embedded, at minimum, at the highest governance level within the company. If the company references a policy belonging to an out-of-scope parent, it records evidence of roll-out, enforcement and accountability mechanisms to support the policy.
