# PSG2.1 (MGPP 2.1) : The company has a mechanism to consider or involve its stakeholders and represent their interests in decision-making.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise dispose d'un mécanisme permettant de prendre en compte ou d'impliquer ses parties prenantes*

Impact Area : Purpose & Stakeholder Governance (Mission et gouvernance des parties prenantes). Pages PDF 169-175. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**PSG2** : The company considers its impacts on stakeholders in decision-making.

**PSG2.1** : The company has a mechanism to consider or involve its stakeholders and represent their interests in decision-making.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.1.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company identifies and prioritizes its stakeholders and maps its stakeholders listed in 2.1.3. .

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 2.1.1 L'entreprise identifie ses parties prenantes, leur attribue un ordre de priorité et dresse la carte de ses parties prenantes énumérées dans le critère de conformité 2.1.3.

### 2.1.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company engages with its stakeholders and considers their interests in decision-making.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 2.1.2 L'entreprise dialogue avec ses parties prenantes et tient compte de leurs intérêts dans ses processus décisionnels.

### 2.1.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The following stakeholders are represented with one or more  engagement mechanisms.

- a) Workers of the company or its subsidiaries
- b) Suppliers
- c) Customers
- d) Investors
- e) The community where offices or facilities of the company, its subsidiaries, its suppliers, or its investments are located
- f) The local or global environment

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 2.1.3 Les parties prenantes suivantes sont représentées par un ou plusieurs mécanismes d'engagement.
a) Les collaborateurs et collaboratrices de l'entreprise ou de ses filiales.
b) Les prestataires de biens et de services.
c) La clientèle.
d) Les partenaires financiers. PAS
e) La communauté où se trouvent les bureaux ou installations de l'entreprise, de ses filiales, de ses prestataires de biens ou de services, ou qui bénéficie de ses investissements.
f) L'environnement local ou mondial.

## Intent

To ensure the company considers the impact of its decision-making on its stakeholders. By doing so, the company puts the B Corp legal requirement into practice.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.1.1] The company’s identification and prioritization of stakeholders includes a comprehensive map of its stakeholders.

[2.1.2] The company describes the process it used to gather and understand stakeholders’ views and interests.

[2.1.3] The company may use direct or indirect engagement mechanisms to consider stakeholders and their interests in its decision-making.

- Direct involvement means engaging stakeholders and considering their interests within the company’s decision-making processes (e.g. making a stakeholder representative a member of the company’s highest governing body).
- Indirect involvement means using various mechanisms to engage stakeholders and consider their interests as input into their decisions.

[2.1.3] The company can use different stakeholder engagement mechanisms flexibly as long as all the stakeholders in the Compliance Criteria are covered.

[2.1.3a] Companies without workers are not expected to consider workers in a stakeholder mechanism.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- References to the highest governing body mean those within the company’s scope of certification. This means that oversight by an out-of-scope parent body does not meet the sub-requirement, unless the company has additional oversight at the highest governing body within its scope of certification.
- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
