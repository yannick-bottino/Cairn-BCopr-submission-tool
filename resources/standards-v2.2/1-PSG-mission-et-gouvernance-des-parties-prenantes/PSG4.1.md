# PSG4.1 (MGPP 4.1) : The company has principles for responsible marketing and public relations practices.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise s'est dotée de principes en matière de marketing et de communication responsables*

Impact Area : Purpose & Stakeholder Governance (Mission et gouvernance des parties prenantes). Pages PDF 229-234. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**PSG4** : The company engages in responsible marketing and public relations.

**PSG4.1** : The company has principles for responsible marketing and public relations practices.

## Compliance criteria

### 4.1.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company documents the following principles governing its marketing and public relations practices related to environmental and social claims.

- a) The company makes precise, verifiable, and substantiated claims based on reliable or scientific data.
- b) The company is truthful, transparent, and accountable about the social and environmental impacts of the company's operations, both positive and negative.
- c)  The company follows ethical guidelines when using sensitive marketing and public relations channels and practices.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 4.1.1 L'entreprise documente les principes suivants régissant ses pratiques de marketing et de communication liées aux allégations environnementales et sociales.
a) Les allégations de l'entreprise sont précises, vérifiables et étayées par des données fiables ou scientifiques.
b) L’entreprise communique de manière transparente, honnête et responsable sur les impacts sociaux et environnementaux de ses activités, qu’ils soient positifs ou négatifs.
c) L’entreprise applique des principes éthiques dans son utilisation des canaux et pratiques de marketing et de communication d'une nature sensible.

### 4.1.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The principles are communicated to company workers and available for them to access.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 4.1.2 Les principes sont communiqués et mis à la disposition des collaborateurs et des collaboratrices de l'entreprise.

## Intent

To ensure the company:

- conducts its marketing and public relations practices responsibly
- considers the impact of its marketing and public relations’ practices on society and the environment.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[4.1.1.a] “Reliable data” means data that is evidence-based and replicable, and includes insights from independent market research.

[4.1.1.a] “Scientific data” means data based on research by scientists which has been published in a reputable peer-reviewed journal.

[4.1.2] The company explains the processes it uses to communicate the responsible marketing and public relations policy (e.g. through onboarding, training, internal guidance, and compliance checks).

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

The commitment is embedded, at minimum, at the highest level within the company. If the company references a commitment belonging to an out-of-scope parent, it records evidence of roll-out, enforcement and accountability mechanisms to support the commitment.
