# PSG2.5 (MGPP 2.5) : The company considers its stakeholders when making decisions about dividends and stock buybacks.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise tient compte de ses parties prenantes dans la prise de décision concernant les dividendes et les rachats d'actions*

Impact Area : Purpose & Stakeholder Governance (Mission et gouvernance des parties prenantes). Pages PDF 199-202. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**PSG2** : The company considers its impacts on stakeholders in decision-making.

**PSG2.5** : The company considers its stakeholders when making decisions about dividends and stock buybacks.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.5.1

The company documents decisions by the highest governing body that relate to dividends and stock buyback and which demonstrate how the company:

- a) considered the interests of its stakeholders (not just shareholders)
- b) evaluated stakeholder interests or balanced conflicting stakeholder interests.

### 2.5.2

The company considers impacts to the following stakeholders in relation to dividend and stock buyback decisions.

- a) Workers of the company or its subsidiaries
- b) Suppliers
- c) Customers
- d) Investors
- e) The community where offices or facilities of the company, its subsidiaries, its suppliers, or its investments are located
- f) The local or global environment

### 2.5.3

The company is publicly transparent on how decisions on dividends and stock buybacks are balanced with reinvestments in the company’s social and environmental performance.

## Intent

To ensure the company puts the B Corp legal requirement for stakeholder governance into practice by:

- considering the impact of its decisions about dividends and stock buybacks on all stakeholders
- avoiding practices that disproportionately benefit certain parties at the expense of others.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.5.1, 2.5.2, 2.5.3] “Dividend” is the distribution of a company's earnings to its shareholders and is typically determined by the company's board of directors. Dividends may be paid out as cash or in the form of reinvestment in additional stock. (Investopedia [EN])

[2.5.1, 2.5.2, 2.5.3] “Stock buyback” is also known as share repurchase; when a company repurchases its own stock, reducing the total number of shares outstanding. (Bankrate [EN])

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- This sub-requirement only applies to companies who have ultimate control over the payout of dividends and decisions regarding stock buybacks.
- References to the highest governing body mean those within the company’s scope of certification.
