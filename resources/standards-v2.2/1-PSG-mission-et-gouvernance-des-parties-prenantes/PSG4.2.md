# PSG4.2 (MGPP 4.2) : The company has a responsible marketing and public relations policy overseen by the executive team or highest governing body.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise dispose d'une politique de marketing et de communication responsables supervisée par l'équipe de direction*

Impact Area : Purpose & Stakeholder Governance (Mission et gouvernance des parties prenantes). Pages PDF 235-242. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**PSG4** : The company engages in responsible marketing and public relations.

**PSG4.2** : The company has a responsible marketing and public relations policy overseen by the executive team or highest governing body.

## Compliance criteria

### 4.2.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company develops a responsible marketing and public relations policy. The policy:

- a) describes its scope
- b) applies to all audiences
- c) outlines requirements for anyone preparing marketing and public relations activities for the company
- d) is approved by the executive team or highest governing body.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 4.2.1 L'entreprise établit une politique de marketing et de communication responsables. Cette politique :
a) décrit le champ d'application de cette politique ;
b) s'applique à tous les publics ;
c) définit les exigences pour toute personne impliquée dans la préparation des activités de marketing et de communication ;
d) est approuvé par l'équipe de direction ou la plus haute instance de direction.

### 4.2.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The requirements in the policy include the following criteria for the company’s social and environmental claims at minimum.

- a) Claims are precise, verifiable, and substantiated using reliable or scientific data.
- b) Claims are proportional to the company's actions.
- c) Claims accurately reflect their applicable scope and are relevant to the company’s impact.
- d) Communications are truthful, transparent, and accountable about the social and environmental impacts of the company’s operations, both positive and negative.
- e) Ethical guidelines govern the company's use of sensitive marketing and public relations’ channels and practices.
- f) Language is understandable and clear, matches the general knowledge level of consumers, and available in prevalent languages or forms relevant to stakeholders where the company operates.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 4.2.2 Concernant les allégations environnementales et sociales, la politique comprend les exigences suivantes au minimum.
a) Les allégations sont précises, vérifiables et étayées par des données fiables ou scientifiques.
b) Les allégations sont proportionnelles aux actions de l'entreprise.
c) Les allégations reflètent avec précision leur champ d'application et sont pertinentes par rapport à l'impact de l'entreprise.
d) La communication est factuelle, transparente et responsable en ce qui concerne les impacts sociaux et environnementaux des activités de l'entreprise, qu'ils soient positifs ou négatifs.
e) L'entreprise dispose de principes éthiques régissant son utilisation des canaux et pratiques de marketing et de communication d'une nature sensible.
f) Les allégations sont formulées de manière claire et compréhensible, adaptées au niveau de connaissances générales des consommateurs, et disponibles dans les principales langues ou sous les formes les plus appropriées pour les parties prenantes des localités où l'entreprise exerce ses activités."y.

### 4.2.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company assigns accountability at the level of the executive team or highest governing body for ensuring all marketing and public relations practices comply with the responsible marketing and public relations policy.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 4.2.3. L'entreprise confie à l'équipe de direction ou à la plus haute instance de direction la responsabilité de veiller à ce que toutes ses pratiques de marketing et de communication soient conformes à sa politique de marketing et de communication responsables.

### 4.2.4

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The policy is communicated to company workers and available for them to access.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 4.2.4 La politique est communiquée aux collaborateurs et collaboratrices de l'entreprise et mise à leur disposition.

## Intent

To ensure the company:

- conducts its marketing and public relations practices responsibly
- reflects its overall social and environmental impact accurately
- considers the broader effects of its marketing and public relations practices on society and the environment.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[4.2.1.b] The policy applies for any audience. The company’s “audience” means  the specific group of people it aims to reach with its messaging products or services.

[4.2.2.a] “Reliable data” means data that is evidence-based and replicable, and includes insights from independent market research.

[4.2.2.a] “Scientific data” means data based on research by scientists which has been published in a reputable peer-reviewed journal.

[4.2.3] Accountability means someone in the executive team or highest governing body oversees responsible marketing and public relations within the company. This role is not necessarily held by someone who carries out day-to-day marketing and public relations activities. The company may assign this responsibility to someone who is not in the highest governing body if they have a relevant dedicated role.

[4.2.4] The company explains the processes it uses to communicate the responsible marketing and public relations policy (e.g. through onboarding, training, internal guidance, and compliance checks).

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The policy is embedded, at minimum, at the highest governance level within the company. If the company references a policy belonging to an out-of-scope parent, it records evidence of roll-out, enforcement and accountability mechanisms to support the policy.
- References to the highest governing body or executive team mean those within the company’s scope of certification. This means that approval by an out-of-scope parent body does not meet the sub-requirement, unless the company has additional approval at the highest governing body or executive team within its scope of certification.
