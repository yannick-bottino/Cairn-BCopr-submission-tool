# PSG3.3 (MGPP 3.3) : The company has a publicly accessible grievance procedure allowing stakeholders to safely raise grievances and seek resolutions.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise dispose d'une procédure de réclamation accessible au public*

Impact Area : Purpose & Stakeholder Governance (Mission et gouvernance des parties prenantes). Pages PDF 215-221. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**PSG3** : The company has adequate procedures to address stakeholder grievances.

**PSG3.3** : The company has a publicly accessible grievance procedure allowing stakeholders to safely raise grievances and seek resolutions.

## Compliance criteria

### 3.3.1

The company’s grievance procedure is available on its webpage in relevant languages or forms to ensure stakeholders can access it.

### 3.3.2

The grievance procedure explains:

- a) grounds for accepting a grievance
- b) grievance process steps and targeted deadlines for managing a grievance
- c) how a resolution is facilitated

### 3.3.3

The grievance procedure states the processes and controls in place to protect stakeholders who raise grievances from any form of retaliation.

### 3.3.4

The company responds to stakeholder grievances by either:

- a) regularly communicating each step in the resolution process and its outcome, and confirming when the grievance has been resolved
- b) explaining why it did not accept the issue as a grievance.

### 3.3.5

The company notifies each of its main stakeholders the relevant grievance procedures.

### 3.3.6

The grievance procedure clearly explains how the decision-making process will avoid conflicts of interest.

## Intent

- To ensure stakeholders have a clear and accessible way to raise concerns and seek resolution about the company’s social, environmental, or governance issues. By enabling this, the company puts the B Corp legal requirement for stakeholder governance into practice.
- To ensure the company has a robust and transparent process for handling grievances.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[3.3.1] As well as having a public grievance procedure on its webpage, the company may need to create alternative procedures to ensure all stakeholders can raise grievances.

[3.3.3] “Protecting stakeholders from any form of retaliation” means implementing measures to enforce confidentiality and assess potential risks to stakeholders. This may include:

- allowing the grievance raiser or other affected stakeholders to request their identities remain confidential if they anticipate retaliation due to the grievance
- clearly informing those raising grievances about who will need to be involved or informed at the outset
- seeking consent before sharing information with additional parties
- maintaining confidentiality regarding the outcome of the process
- enhanced training for those handling the grievance procedure
- enforcing disciplinary action in cases of confidentiality breaches
- collaborating with independent third parties to assess risks for vulnerable stakeholders.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

The grievance procedure is embedded, at minimum, at the highest level within the company. If the company references a grievance procedure belonging to an out-of-scope parent, it records evidence of roll-out, enforcement and accountability mechanisms to support the grievance procedure.
