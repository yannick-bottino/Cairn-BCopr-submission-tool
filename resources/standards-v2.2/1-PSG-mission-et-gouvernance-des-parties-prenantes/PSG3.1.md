# PSG3.1 (MGPP 3.1) : The company has a publicly accessible grievance procedure allowing stakeholders to safely raise grievances and seek resolutions.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise dispose d'une procédure de réclamation accessible au public*

Impact Area : Purpose & Stakeholder Governance (Mission et gouvernance des parties prenantes). Pages PDF 203-209. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**PSG3** : The company has adequate procedures to address stakeholder grievances.

**PSG3.1** : The company has a publicly accessible grievance procedure allowing stakeholders to safely raise grievances and seek resolutions.

## Compliance criteria

### 3.1.1

The company publishes a grievance form on its webpage or has another easily accessible way for stakeholders to raise a grievance.

### 3.1.2

The grievance procedure explains:

- a) grounds for accepting a grievance
- b) grievance process steps and targeted deadlines for managing a grievance
- c) how a resolution is facilitated

### 3.1.3

The company describes the processes and controls in place to protect stakeholders who raise grievances from any form of retaliation.

### 3.1.4

The company responds to stakeholder grievances by either:

- a) regularly communicating each step in the resolution process and its outcome to the complainant, and confirming when the grievance has been resolved
- b) explaining why it did not accept the issue as a grievance.

## Intent

To ensure stakeholders have a clear and accessible way to raise concerns and seek resolution about the company’s social, environmental, or governance issues. By enabling this, the company puts the B Corp legal requirement for stakeholder governance into practice.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[3.1.1] The company’s membership in an industry or sector that provides a grievance procedure to its members may qualify, as long as the procedure still meets the compliance criteria.

[3.1.1] If the company does not publish a grievance form and procedure on its website, it makes other methods for raising a grievance transparent, easily accessible and ongoing. These may include:

- a general email address for all queries
- a whistleblower or ethics hotline
- business-to-business contract clauses with dispute resolution provisions
- “Open-door” or “speak up” policies for workers
- suggestion or feedback boxes, or other worker voice tools
- direct stakeholder engagement
- worker committees
- a customer services department
- trade unions and industrial relations processes
- consumer complaints mechanisms
- Code of Conduct requirements for supplier mechanisms
- community outreach and engagement.

[3.1.3] Protecting stakeholders from any form of retaliation means implementing measures to enforce confidentiality and assess potential risks to stakeholders. This may include:

- allowing the grievance raiser or other affected stakeholders to request their identities remain confidential if they anticipate retaliation due to the grievance
- clearly informing those raising grievances about who will need to be involved or informed at the outset
- seeking consent before sharing information with additional parties
- maintaining confidentiality regarding the outcome of the process
- enhanced training for those handling the grievance procedure
- enforcing disciplinary action in cases of confidentiality breaches
- collaborating with independent third parties to assess risks for vulnerable stakeholders.

[3.1.4] “Regularly communicating” about a grievance means updating the stakeholder as needed about the process. This may include providing information about:

- the decision-making process
- the timelines for reviewing the grievance
- the scope of grievances the company has accepted
- the company’s resolution management approach
- the grievance being closed
- any other information useful for the stakeholder.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

The grievance procedure is embedded, at minimum, at the highest level within the company. If the company references a grievance procedure belonging to an out-of-scope parent, it records evidence of roll-out, enforcement and accountability mechanisms to support the grievance procedure.
