# PSG5.1 (MGPP 5.1) : The highest governing body monitors the company's purpose, social and environmental impact, and stakeholder considerations.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : La plus haute instance de direction supervise le déploiement de la raison d'être et la gestion de l'impact*

Impact Area : Purpose & Stakeholder Governance (Mission et gouvernance des parties prenantes). Pages PDF 243-249. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**PSG5** : The company’s social and environmental impact and stakeholder considerations are monitored by the highest governing body and integrated throughout the company.

**PSG5.1** : The highest governing body monitors the company's purpose, social and environmental impact, and stakeholder considerations.

## Compliance criteria

### 5.1.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

At least once every 12 months, the highest governing body reviews the company’s:

- a) progress on advancing its public purpose as defined under PSG1.1
- b) social and environmental performance
- c) stakeholder governance implementation.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 5.1.1. Au moins une fois tous les 12 mois  la plus haute instance de direction examine :
a) Les progrès accomplis dans le déploiement de la raison d'être de l'entreprise, telle que définie dans la sous-exigence MGPP1.1 ;
b) Les performances sociales et environnementales de l'entreprise ;
c) Le déploiement de la gouvernance des parties prenantes.

## Intent

To ensure the highest governing body is accountable for advancing the company purpose, improving its social and environmental performance, and overseeing the implementation of stakeholder governance. By creating this accountability, the company puts the B Corp legal requirement for stakeholder governance into practice.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[5.1.1] The company can use stakeholder interests or its materiality assessments to identify the social and environmental topics that require oversight from the highest governing body.

[5.1.1] The following sub-requirements of the B Lab Standard require, at minimum, annual updates or monitoring by the highest governing body.

- Public reporting of lobbying activity
- Considering stakeholders
- Grievance procedure reporting
- Long-term social or environmental goal
- Impact reporting

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- References to the highest governing body mean those within the company’s scope of certification. This means that oversight by an out-of-scope parent body does not meet the sub-requirement, unless the company has additional oversight at the highest governing body within its scope of certification.
