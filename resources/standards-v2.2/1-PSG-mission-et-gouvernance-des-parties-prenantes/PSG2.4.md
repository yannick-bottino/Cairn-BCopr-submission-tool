# PSG2.4 (MGPP 2.4) : The company identifies material topics not addressed in the B Lab Standard, sets targets, and reports progress annually.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise identifie les enjeux matériels non couverts par les standards de B Lab*

Impact Area : Purpose & Stakeholder Governance (Mission et gouvernance des parties prenantes). Pages PDF 193-198. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**PSG2** : The company considers its impacts on stakeholders in decision-making.

**PSG2.4** : The company identifies material topics not addressed in the B Lab Standard, sets targets, and reports progress annually.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.4.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company identifies any material topics from its materiality assessment that are not addressed in the B Lab Standard.

### 2.4.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company sets at least one target per material topic it identifies.

### 2.4.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The targets are:

- a) specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART)
- b) integrated into the company strategy
- c) available to the public
- d) approved by the highest governing body

### 2.4.4

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company assigns responsibility for achieving each target.

### 2.4.5

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

In the years after setting its targets, the company publicly reports its progress in its impact report or website.

### 2.4.6

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

Once the company has achieved a target for a material topic, it sets a new target that meets these Compliance Criteria.

## Intent

To ensure the company:

- acts on material topics that may not be addressed in the B Lab Standard.
- is accountable to its commitments and stakeholders, through:
  - transparency (i.e. sharing targets and progress publicly)
  - highest governing body oversight.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.4.1] The company identifies its material topics under PSG2.3.

[2.4.1]. The company uses a gap analysis to identify material topics not addressed in the B Lab Standard. This involves the company comparing the material topics it prioritized in its assessment (PSG2.3) with the B Lab Standard. This sub-requirement applies for all material topics the company has identified that are not addressed in the B Lab Standard.

[2.4.1] If all of the company’s material topics are addressed in the B Lab Impact Topics requirements, the company meets this sub-requirement by setting at least one target related to a material topic that goes beyond the actions required in the B Lab Standard. The target meets all Compliance Criteria in this sub-requirement.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
- References to the highest governing body mean those within the company’s scope of certification. This means that oversight by an out-of-scope parent body does not meet the sub-requirement, unless the company has additional oversight at the highest governing body within its scope of certification.
