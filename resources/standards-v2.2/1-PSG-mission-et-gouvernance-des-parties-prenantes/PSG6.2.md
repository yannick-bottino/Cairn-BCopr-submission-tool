# PSG6.2 (MGPP 6.2) : The company reports its social and environmental performance annually and publicly using a third-party standard.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : Chaque année, l'entreprise rend compte publiquement en s'appuyant sur un standard externe*

Impact Area : Purpose & Stakeholder Governance (Mission et gouvernance des parties prenantes). Pages PDF 277-281. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**PSG6** : The company is transparent about its social and environmental performance.

**PSG6.2** : The company reports its social and environmental performance annually and publicly using a third-party standard.

## Compliance criteria

### 6.2.1

The company’s social and environmental impact reports

- a) uses a third-party standard
- b) is approved by the highest governing body
- c) is published on the company’s webpage and made accessible to all stakeholders
- d) is prepared and published at least annually
- e) follows a consistent structure to show progress over the years.

### 6.2.2

The company’s social and environmental impact report includes:

- a) progress against all relevant measures of the company’s social and environmental performance and a summary of stakeholder engagement processes
- b) what concrete actions the company has taken
- c) the management strategies the company is using to achieve its social and environmental goals and its progress towards them.

## Intent

To transparently communicate the company’s social and environmental performance. By doing so, the company:

- demonstrates accountability
- shares progress toward its goals
- upholds its commitment to stakeholders
- provides insights into its impact and strategies for improvement.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[6.2.1.a] The Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) is the most widely used standard for impact reporting. Using GRI is not mandatory — however, if the company uses another third-party standard, it needs to be reviewed by B Lab’s Standards Management team. Their review will ensure the third-party standard:

- is credible
- is consistent
- provides comprehensive coverage.

[6.2.1.a] An impact report following the European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS) would also meet this criteria.

[6.2.1.a] The following actions do not meet the sub-requirement.

- Producing a non-financial report with sustainability metrics the company has created, or using a third-party (such as an accounting firm) to audit information would not meet the third-party standard requirement. While this type of audit confirms the accuracy of information, it cannot determine whether that information adheres to comparable standards like GRI.
- Participating in a third-party reporting initiative, such as the CDP or the Dow Jones Sustainability Index, does not meet this sub-requirement.

While these initiatives are valuable, they are not focused on broad-based public transparency for all stakeholders.

[6.2.1.c] “Accessible to all stakeholders” means stakeholders can easily navigate to the report from the company’s homepage.

[6.2.2.a] The report is comprehensive. It addresses the company’s

- key social and environmental issues, and the actions it has taken
- social and environmental performance metrics
- progress toward targets
- consideration of stakeholder interests.

[6.2.2.a] “Stakeholder engagement” in this sub-requirement refers to gathering input from stakeholders to help define material topics [Link to PSG2.3].

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The reporting scope covers the company. If the company references a corporate group impact report that includes out-of-scope entities, either:
  - the performance of the company is clearly identifiable within the group impact report
  - the company publicly shares its own impact report on its webpage.
- References to the highest governing body mean those within the company’s scope of certification. This means that approval by an out-of-scope parent body does not meet the sub-requirement, unless the company has additional approval at the highest governing body or executive team within its scope of certification.
