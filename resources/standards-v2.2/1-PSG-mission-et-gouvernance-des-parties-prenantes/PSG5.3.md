# PSG5.3 (MGPP 5.3) : All members of the executive team have at least one annual target tied to the company’s social or environmental performance.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : Tous les membres de l'équipe de direction ont au moins un objectif annuel lié aux performances sociales ou environnementales*

Impact Area : Purpose & Stakeholder Governance (Mission et gouvernance des parties prenantes). Pages PDF 255-259. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**PSG5** : The company’s social and environmental impact and stakeholder considerations are monitored by the highest governing body and integrated throughout the company.

**PSG5.3** : All members of the executive team have at least one annual target tied to the company’s social or environmental performance.

## Compliance criteria

### 5.3.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

All members of the company’s executive team have at least one annual target tied to the company’s social or environmental performance.

### 5.3.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The targets are SMART (specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound).

## Intent

To establish accountability among company leadership for enhancing the company's social and environmental performance. By doing so, the company puts the B Corp legal requirement into practice.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[5.3.1] In some companies, the executive team is the highest governing body; in others, the executive team implements the strategy set by the highest governing body. The two groups may or may not overlap. This sub-requirement applies to the executive team, if the company does not have a separate highest governing body.

[5.3.2] The targets are SMART to ensure they can be audited. They are linked to the company’s strategy and material topics, based on its materiality assessment or the B Corp Standard, or both. The company reviews the targets as needed to ensure they remain relevant and effective.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- References to the executive team are within the scope of certification of the company. This means that the company may refer to an out-of-scope parent executive team targets or create their own targets as long as it also applies to the executive team within the scope of certification.
