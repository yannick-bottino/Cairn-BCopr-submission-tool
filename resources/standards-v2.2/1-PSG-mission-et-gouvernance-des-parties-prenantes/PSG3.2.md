# PSG3.2 (MGPP 3.2) : The company tracks grievances and assigns accountability for resolving them.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise recense les réclamations et attribue la responsabilité de leur résolution*

Impact Area : Purpose & Stakeholder Governance (Mission et gouvernance des parties prenantes). Pages PDF 210-214. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**PSG3** : The company has adequate procedures to address stakeholder grievances.

**PSG3.2** : The company tracks grievances and assigns accountability for resolving them.

## Compliance criteria

### 3.2.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company monitors and tracks grievances and prepares annual summaries of the status and subject of any grievances.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 3.2.1 L'entreprise recense les réclamations, suit leur évolution et prépare un résumé annuel qui récapitule leur statut et les sujets concernés.

### 3.2.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company has an employee with well-defined responsibilities for managing grievances.

### 3.2.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company demonstrates the effectiveness of the grievance procedure by either:

- a) recording evidence that a grievance has been resolved and closed
- b) demonstrating that they have the appropriate grievance procedure in place (inclusive of tracking), where a company has had no grievances filed.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 3.2.3 L'entreprise démontre l'efficacité de la procédure de réclamation d'une des manières suivantes.
a) Elle documente la résolution et la clôture de chaque réclamation.
b) Si aucune réclamation n'a été enregistrée, l'entreprise démontre que la procédure en place est adéquate et qu'elle permet notamment le recensement et le suivi des réclamations.

## Intent

To ensure the company is accountable to grievances raised by stakeholders about the company’s social, environmental, or governance issues.

By enabling this, the company puts the B Corp legal requirement for stakeholder governance into practice.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[3.2.1] The company's summary of grievances includes:

- status (under review, active, or closed)
- grievance case reference
- the date the grievance was filed
- who raised the grievance (unless they have requested confidentiality)
- the defendant
- the location of the grievance (if it is not sensitive)
- the nature of the grievance.

[3.2.2] The responsibility for managing grievances may be part of one employee’s wider role, or shared across various roles — as long as the company is accountable to its grievance procedure overall.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The grievance procedure is embedded, at minimum, at the highest level within the company. If the company references a grievance procedure belonging to an out-of-scope parent, it records evidence of roll-out, enforcement and accountability mechanisms to support the grievance procedure.
- References to roles and responsibilities mean those within the company’s scope of certification. This means that roles and responsibilities of an out-of-scope parent body do not meet the sub-requirement, unless the company has additional roles and responsibilities within its scope of certification.
