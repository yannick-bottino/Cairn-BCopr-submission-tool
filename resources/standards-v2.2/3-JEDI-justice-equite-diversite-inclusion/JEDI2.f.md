# JEDI2.f (JEDI 2.f) : The company supports at least two employee resource or affinity groups. [Within the Workplace]

Impact Area : Justice, Equity, Diversity & Inclusion (Justice, équité, diversité & inclusion). Pages PDF 481-484. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**JEDI2** : The company chooses and implements JEDI actions.

**JEDI2.f** : The company supports at least two employee resource or affinity groups. [Within the Workplace]

## Compliance criteria

### 2.f.1

The company supports at least two employee resource or affinity groups. Each group is associated with a different social identity.

### 2.f.2

The company chooses the social identities using worker feedback.

### 2.f.3

Participation in the groups is optional for workers.

### 2.f.4

The company supports the groups.

## Intent

To create space for workers with common identities, interests, and challenges to discuss and share.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.f.1, 2.f.4] Both types of groups are worker-led, meaning they are empowered to decide how and how often they meet, and whether or not to take notes.

[2.f.1] Employee resource groups and affinity groups are associated with a particular social identity.
