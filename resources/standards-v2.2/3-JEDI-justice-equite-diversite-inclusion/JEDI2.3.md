# JEDI2.3 (JEDI 2.3) : The company chooses its JEDI actions using data and stakeholder feedback, records them in a plan, and implements them.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise choisit ses actions JEDI sur la base des données et des retours des parties prenantes, les consigne dans un plan et les met en œuvre.*

Impact Area : Justice, Equity, Diversity & Inclusion (Justice, équité, diversité & inclusion). Pages PDF 442-445. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**JEDI2** : The company chooses and implements JEDI actions.

**JEDI2.3** : The company chooses its JEDI actions using data and stakeholder feedback, records them in a plan, and implements them.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.3.1

The company chooses and implements:

- a) two JEDI actions before Year 0
- b) four JEDI actions for Years 3 and 5 each.

### 2.3.2

The company chooses at minimum one option from each set across Years 0, 3, and 5.

- a) Foundation (JEDI2.a-e)
- b) Within the Workplace (JEDI2.f-l)
- c) Beyond the Workplace (JEDI2.m-s)

### 2.3.3

The company considers stakeholder feedback and data (from JEDI1) when choosing its actions.

### 2.3.4

The company records its actions and status updates in a plan.

### 2.3.5

The plan was updated and shared with relevant stakeholders in the last twelve months.

### 2.3.6

The company’s plan:

- a) contains deadlines
- b) allocates resources
- c) assigns responsibilities to specific roles.

### 2.3.7

The plan, including status updates, is available for workers to review at any time

## Intent

To ensure all companies implement JEDI actions relevant to their context.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.3.2] The company has implemented at minimum one action from each set when looking at all the actions implemented for Years 0, 3, and 5.

The company does not need to implement an action from each set per Year.

[2.3.2] The company may choose the same option multiple times across Years 0, 3, and 5. For example, the company may continuously commit to JEDI principles and therefore meet JEDI2.a in Years 0, 3, and 5.

[2.3.3] The company’s “relevant stakeholders” are those whose feedback the company considered when choosing its actions. Workers are always considered relevant stakeholders.

[2.3.3] The company may obtain “stakeholder feedback” through primary or secondary sources.

- “Primary sources” refers to feedback directly from stakeholders.
- “Secondary sources” include, for example, reports and news articles.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
