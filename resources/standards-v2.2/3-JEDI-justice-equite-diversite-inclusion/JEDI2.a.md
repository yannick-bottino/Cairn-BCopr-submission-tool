# JEDI2.a (JEDI 2.a) : The company commits publicly to JEDI principles. [Foundation]

Impact Area : Justice, Equity, Diversity & Inclusion (Justice, équité, diversité & inclusion). Pages PDF 458-461. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**JEDI2** : The company chooses and implements JEDI actions.

**JEDI2.a** : The company commits publicly to JEDI principles. [Foundation]

## Compliance criteria

### 2.a.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company develops a JEDI commitment statement that:

- a) is public
- b) is approved by the executive team or highest governing body
- c) applies to the company's policies and practices across operations, customers, and community.

### 2.a.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company assigns accountability for the JEDI commitment to the executive team or highest governing body.

## Intent

To increase internal and public accountability for JEDI commitments.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.a.1] The company is not required to use the term “JEDI”.

[2.a.1] Human rights or human resources policies do not meet this sub-requirement, unless they explicitly incorporate JEDI principles. For example, a human rights policy that describes the importance of JEDI principles and how they relate to the rights of all people would be acceptable.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The commitment is embedded, at minimum, at the highest level within the company. If the company references a commitment belonging to an out-of-scope parent, it records evidence of roll-out, enforcement and accountability mechanisms to support the commitment.
- References to the highest governing body or executive team mean those within the company’s scope of certification. This means that approval by an out-of-scope parent body does not meet the sub-requirement, unless the company has additional approval at the highest governing body or executive team within its scope of certification.
