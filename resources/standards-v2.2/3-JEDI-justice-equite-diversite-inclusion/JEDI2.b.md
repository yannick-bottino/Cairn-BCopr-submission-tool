# JEDI2.b (JEDI 2.b) : The company improves JEDI knowledge and capacity among leaders. [Foundation]

Impact Area : Justice, Equity, Diversity & Inclusion (Justice, équité, diversité & inclusion). Pages PDF 462-466. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**JEDI2** : The company chooses and implements JEDI actions.

**JEDI2.b** : The company improves JEDI knowledge and capacity among leaders. [Foundation]

## Compliance criteria

### 2.b.1

The company carries out training or a workshop on JEDI-related topics. At minimum, the training or workshop amounts to one full day per participant within a one-year period.

### 2.b.2

The company improves the JEDI knowledge or capacity of at least two of the following groups.

- a) The highest governing body
- b) The executive team
- c) Human resources
- d) Training & development
- e) Managers

## Intent

To better equip leaders to implement JEDI actions.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.b.1] Training and workshops may be carried out by an internal or external facilitator.

[2.b.1] Facilitating discussions on JEDI principles as part of JEDI1.1 does not count toward this sub-requirement.

[2.b.1] The company is not required to use the term JEDI.

[2.b.2] The company “improves knowledge” by carrying out training or workshops.

[2.b.2] The company “improves capacity” by recruiting people with expertise in JEDI.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
