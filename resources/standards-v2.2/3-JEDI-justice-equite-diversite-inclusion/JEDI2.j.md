# JEDI2.j (JEDI 2.j) : The company increases its proportion of workers from underrepresented groups to reflect the diversity of its community. [Within the Workplace]

Impact Area : Justice, Equity, Diversity & Inclusion (Justice, équité, diversité & inclusion). Pages PDF 497-501. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**JEDI2** : The company chooses and implements JEDI actions.

**JEDI2.j** : The company increases its proportion of workers from underrepresented groups to reflect the diversity of its community. [Within the Workplace]

## Compliance criteria

### 2.j.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company increases the proportion of workers from at least two underrepresented groups to reflect the diversity of its community.

### 2.j.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company calculates the ratios at any point in the last twelve months.

## Intent

To reflect the diversity of the company’s community in its workforce.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.j.1; 2.j.2] Matching the ratios of all three chosen social identities exactly is likely impossible, particularly if the workforce is small. The company can therefore meet this sub-requirement by being within five percentage points of the target.

[2.j.1] The demographics of the company’s communities may refer to demographics in the company’s immediate surroundings (e.g. province or state) or country.
