# JEDI2.i (JEDI 2.i) : The company develops and implements an inclusive language guide for internal communications. [Within the Workplace]

Impact Area : Justice, Equity, Diversity & Inclusion (Justice, équité, diversité & inclusion). Pages PDF 493-496. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**JEDI2** : The company chooses and implements JEDI actions.

**JEDI2.i** : The company develops and implements an inclusive language guide for internal communications. [Within the Workplace]

## Compliance criteria

### 2.i.1

The company develops an inclusive language guide for internal communications.

### 2.i.2

The guide lists terms that should and should not be used.

### 2.i.3

One guide exists for each internal communication language.

### 2.i.4

The company shares the guide with all workers who produce internal communications.

## Intent

To ensure the company has guidance on inclusive communication for internal communications.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.i.1] The company may adopt a guide made by another organization instead of developing its own.

[2.i.1] The company may use one document as both its:

- internal inclusive language guide (JEDI2.i)
- external inclusive language and ethical content guide (JEDI2.p).

[2.i.1] The company is not required to use the term “inclusive” or “JEDI”.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
