# JEDI2.q (JEDI 2.q) : The company assesses how inclusive a product or service is. [Beyond the Workplace]

Impact Area : Justice, Equity, Diversity & Inclusion (Justice, équité, diversité & inclusion). Pages PDF 527-529. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**JEDI2** : The company chooses and implements JEDI actions.

**JEDI2.q** : The company assesses how inclusive a product or service is. [Beyond the Workplace]

## Compliance criteria

### 2.q.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company uses stakeholder feedback to assess a product or service for inclusivity.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 2.q.1 L'entreprise s'appuie sur les retours des parties prenantes pour évaluer l'inclusivité d'un produit ou d'un service.

### 2.q.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The assessment highlights:

- a) which specific social identity groups may experience barriers when accessing the product or service
- b) what those barriers are.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 2.q.2 L'évaluation met en évidence :
a) Quels sont les groupes d'identité sociale spécifiques susceptibles de rencontrer des obstacles lors de l'accès au produit ou au service ;
b) Quels sont ces obstacles.

## Intent

To increase the company’s understanding of the barriers to accessing one of its products or services.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.q.1] A “product” is one of the company’s finished goods. How inclusive the product is depends on the end consumers’ experience. This may mean this sub-requirement is not applicable if the company produces raw materials or semi-finished goods (e.g. fruit or steel), as it has less control over the end-consumer’s experience.

[2.q.1] A “service” may be any of the company’s services. How inclusive the service is depends on the experience of the user (e.g. the client).

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The assessment covers the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate assessment, it records evidence of:
  - how the assessment considered its stakeholders and operational impacts
  - how the assessment outcome is relevant to its context.
