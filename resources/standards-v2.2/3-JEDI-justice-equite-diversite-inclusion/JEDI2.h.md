# JEDI2.h (JEDI 2.h) : The company provides sponsorship and mentorship opportunities to employees. [Within the Workplace]

Impact Area : Justice, Equity, Diversity & Inclusion (Justice, équité, diversité & inclusion). Pages PDF 488-492. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**JEDI2** : The company chooses and implements JEDI actions.

**JEDI2.h** : The company provides sponsorship and mentorship opportunities to employees. [Within the Workplace]

## Compliance criteria

### 2.h.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company provides sponsorship or mentorship opportunities to all employees.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 2.h.1 L'entreprise offre des possibilités de parrainage ou de mentorat à l'ensemble de ses salariés.

### 2.h.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company records the formal nature of the sponsorship or mentorship program in a procedure or policy document.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 2.h.2 L'entreprise formalise le programme de parrainage ou de mentorat en l'inscrivant dans une procédure ou une politique écrite.

### 2.h.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The sponsorships or mentorships:

- a) are free for participants
- b) last six months at minimum.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 2.h.3 Les parrainages ou les mentorats :
a) Sont gratuits pour les participantes et participants;
b) Durent six mois au minimum.

### 2.h.4

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company gives participants time during standard working hours to participate.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 2.h.4 L’entreprise permet aux participantes et participants de prendre part à l’initiative sur leur temps de travail.

### 2.h.5

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company tracks participation of two underrepresented groups.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 2.h.5 L'entreprise suit le taux de participation de deux groupes sous-représentés.

### 2.h.6

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company achieves representative participation of at least two underrepresented groups.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 2.h.6 L'entreprise atteint un niveau de participation représentative pour au moins deux groupes sous-représentés.

## Intent

To provide tailored support and guidance to advance employees’ personal and professional development.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.h.5; 2.h.6] The company identifies “underrepresented groups” by comparing the proportion of a group in a sample with a population. For example, the company may compare its proportion of:

- women employees with women in the country
- women managers with women employees.

[2.h.5; 2.h.6] “Achieving representative participation” involves matching or exceeding the target proportion. For example, if 20% of the workforce identifies as having a disability, then 20% of participants in the sponsorship or mentorship program identify as having a disability.

[2.h.5] “Tracking participation of two underrepresented groups” means the company:

- chooses two underrepresented groups to track (e.g. women and persons with disabilities)
- records how many people from those groups are  participating in the sponsorship and mentorship opportunities.
- women managers with women employees.
