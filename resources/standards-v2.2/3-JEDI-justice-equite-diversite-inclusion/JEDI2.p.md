# JEDI2.p (JEDI 2.p) : The company develops and implements an inclusive communications and ethical content guide for external communications. [Beyond the Workplace]

Impact Area : Justice, Equity, Diversity & Inclusion (Justice, équité, diversité & inclusion). Pages PDF 522-526. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**JEDI2** : The company chooses and implements JEDI actions.

**JEDI2.p** : The company develops and implements an inclusive communications and ethical content guide for external communications. [Beyond the Workplace]

## Compliance criteria

### 2.p.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company develops a communications and ethical content guide that:

- a) lists terms that should and should not be used for each external communication language
- b) describes how to gather, manage, and use content ethically
- c) sets expectations for representing social identities
- d) is shared with all workers who produce external communications.

### 2.p.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

If the company uses artificial intelligence for creative content generation, it includes its use in the guide.

## Intent

To ensure the company has guidance on inclusive communication and ethical content management for external communications.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.p.1] “Content” includes images, videos, and people’s stories.

[2.p.1] The company may adopt a guide made by another organization instead of developing its own.

[2.p.1] The company is not required to use the terms “inclusive” or “JEDI”.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
