# JEDI2.o (JEDI 2.o) : The company promotes diversity among its local suppliers. [Beyond the Workplace]

Impact Area : Justice, Equity, Diversity & Inclusion (Justice, équité, diversité & inclusion). Pages PDF 518-521. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**JEDI2** : The company chooses and implements JEDI actions.

**JEDI2.o** : The company promotes diversity among its local suppliers. [Beyond the Workplace]

## Compliance criteria

### 2.o.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company implements all of the following actions.

- a) It tracks diversity of ownership among its local suppliers.
- b) It establishes a policy to give preferences to local suppliers owned by persons from underrepresented groups.
- c) It sets targets to purchase a percentage of goods and services from local suppliers owned by persons from underrepresented groups.

## Intent

To improve opportunities for underrepresented groups in the company’s supply chain.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.o.1] “Local suppliers” means suppliers owned and operated within the same community of the company or relevant facilities in which the company operates. This excludes local branches of suppliers headquartered elsewhere. The size and distance of the company’s community may vary by context, but should generally be based on a small-scale, economically- and culturally-connected area — like a metropolitan area, or a county in rural settings — and its vicinity (usually within 75 kilometers, or 50 miles).

[2.o.1 a] To “track diversity of ownership”, the company maintains a list of local supplier ownership and whether they identify as part of an underrepresented group. The company is not expected to have this information for all local suppliers, as some may not feel comfortable sharing it.

[2.o.1 b] The company defines the underrepresented groups, as they are context-dependent.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
