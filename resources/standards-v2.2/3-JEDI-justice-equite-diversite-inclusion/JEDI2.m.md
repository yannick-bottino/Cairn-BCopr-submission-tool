# JEDI2.m (JEDI 2.m) : The company’s website meets accessibility standards. [Beyond the Workplace]

Impact Area : Justice, Equity, Diversity & Inclusion (Justice, équité, diversité & inclusion). Pages PDF 510-513. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**JEDI2** : The company chooses and implements JEDI actions.

**JEDI2.m** : The company’s website meets accessibility standards. [Beyond the Workplace]

## Compliance criteria

### 2.m.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company assesses the accessibility of its public-facing website.

### 2.m.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The assessment:

- a) confirms the website meets the AA or AAA criteria of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG)
- b) is carried out by an internal or external specialist
- c) includes manual testing
- d) includes feedback from people with disabilities.

## Intent

To ensure the company’s website is accessible to people with disabilities.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.m.1] The company uses the latest version of WCAG available in its preferred language. The company does not need to comply with draft versions of WCAG, however it can meet the sub-requirement by using draft versions.

[2.m.2 c] “Manual testing” means testing or assessing done by a person — not an automated testing tool. An assessment based on automatic testing alone does not meet this sub-requirement.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
