# JEDI2.n (JEDI 2.n) : The company communicates its JEDI action plan and progress publicly. [Beyond the Workplace]

Impact Area : Justice, Equity, Diversity & Inclusion (Justice, équité, diversité & inclusion). Pages PDF 514-517. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**JEDI2** : The company chooses and implements JEDI actions.

**JEDI2.n** : The company communicates its JEDI action plan and progress publicly. [Beyond the Workplace]

## Compliance criteria

### 2.n.1

The company communicates its JEDI action plan and progress publicly, once per year at minimum.

## Intent

To increase internal and public accountability for JEDI actions.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.n.1] The company’s “JEDI action plan” is the action plan resulting from these requirement options in the B Lab Standard (JEDI2.3-2.6), which apply to Small companies and larger. The company may add other actions not required by the Standard.

[2.n.1] The company shares both its forward progress and its lack of progress — where it has encountered challenges or failed to meet commitments.

[2.n.1] The company is not required to use the term “JEDI”.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
