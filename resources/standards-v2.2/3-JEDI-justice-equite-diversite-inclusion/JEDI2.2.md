# JEDI2.2 (JEDI 2.2) : The company chooses its JEDI actions and implements them.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise choisit ses actions JEDI et les met en œuvre.*

Impact Area : Justice, Equity, Diversity & Inclusion (Justice, équité, diversité & inclusion). Pages PDF 439-441. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**JEDI2** : The company chooses and implements JEDI actions.

**JEDI2.2** : The company chooses its JEDI actions and implements them.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.2.1

The company chooses and implements:

- a) two JEDI actions before Year 0
- b) three JEDI actions for Years 3 and 5 each.

### 2.2.2

The company chooses and implements at minimum one option from each set across Years 0, 3, and 5.

- a) Foundation (JEDI2.a-e)
- b) Within the Workplace (JEDI2.f-l)
- c) Beyond the Workplace (JEDI2.m-s)

## Intent

To ensure all companies implement JEDI actions relevant to their context.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.2.2] The company has implemented at minimum one action from each set when looking at all the actions implemented for Years 0, 3, and 5.

The company does not need to implement an action from each set per Year.

[2.2.2] The company may choose the same option multiple times across Years 0, 3, and 5. For example, the company may continuously commit to JEDI principles and therefore meet JEDI2.a in Years 0, 3, and 5.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
