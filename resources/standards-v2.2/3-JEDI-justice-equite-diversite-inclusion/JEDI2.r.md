# JEDI2.r (JEDI 2.r) : The company redesigns a product or service to be more inclusive. [Beyond the Workplace]

Impact Area : Justice, Equity, Diversity & Inclusion (Justice, équité, diversité & inclusion). Pages PDF 530-533. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**JEDI2** : The company chooses and implements JEDI actions.

**JEDI2.r** : The company redesigns a product or service to be more inclusive. [Beyond the Workplace]

## Compliance criteria

### 2.r.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company uses stakeholder feedback to redesign one of its products or services to be more inclusive.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 2.r.1 L'Entreprise s'appuie sur les retours des parties prenantes pour revoir la conception de l'un de ses produits ou services afin qu'il soit plus inclusif.

### 2.r.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company redesigns the original product or service, rather than designing a segregation solution.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 2.r.2 L'entreprise repense le produit ou le service original, plutôt que de concevoir une solution de ségrégation.

### 2.r.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company offers the redesigned product or service to customers or announces it publicly.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 2.r.3 L'entreprise propose le produit ou le service reconçu à sa clientèle ou l'annonce publiquement.

## Intent

To eliminate barriers to accessing a product or service.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.r.1] A “product” is one of the company’s finished goods. How inclusive the product is depends on the end consumers’ experience. This may mean this sub-requirement is not applicable if the company produces raw materials or semi-finished goods (e.g. fruit or steel), as it has less control over the end-consumer’s experience.

[2.r.1] A “service” may be any of the company’s services. How inclusive the service is depends on the experience of the user (e.g. the client).

[2.r.2] A “segregation solution” means developing a separate, specialized, or segregated version for people who cannot access the original product or service (Inclusive Design Research Centre). For example, if a camera sensor is more effective at detecting lighter skin tones than darker ones, creating a second sensor — specifically for darker skin tones — would be a segregation solution. Instead, under this sub-requirement, the company redesigns the original sensor to work for all skin tones. Redesigning for inclusivity, rather than segregation, is generally preferred.

Some exceptions may apply — for example, if a product cannot work for multiple groups (e.g. contraception solutions may only work for one sex).

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
