# FR3.1.l (EB 3.1.l) : [Your operations] In the past fiscal year, did the company operate a water-intensive production in an area with water risk?

Impact Area : Foundation Requirements (Exigences de base). Pages PDF 125-131. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**FR3** : The company creates a risk profile and meets any additional sub-requirements.

**FR3.1.l** : [Your operations] In the past fiscal year, did the company operate a water-intensive production in an area with water risk?

## Compliance criteria

### 3.1.l.1

[Small companies in Manufacturing, Agriculture, Retail/Wholesale, or Service with a significant footprint] If Yes, in addition to the sub-requirements that already apply, the company meets the following criteria.

- a) The company meets ESC1.7, ESC2.1 and ESC4.2.
- b) The company considers the potential impact (see Intent) in their assessment of actual and potential environmental impacts (ESC1.7).
- c) The company selects “not applicable” for ESC4.1 because ESC4.2 replaces it.

## Intent

- Potential Impact: Negative environmental impacts related to water-intensive production in areas with water risk

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

- The scope is the company's own operations
- Area with water risk: Covers several physical aspects related to water, including water availability; quality; quantity (including areas at high water-stress); accessibility of water; regulatory or reputational issues (including the shared use of water with communities and affordability of water) for its facilities and for the facilities of key suppliers. (European Sustainability Reporting Standards E2, 2023)
- Physical water risks may include, for example:
  - physical quantity risk, related to too little or too much water (e.g. drought risk, water stress, water depletion, flood)
  - physical quality risk, related to water that is unfit for use (e.g. water contamination, untreated connected wastewater)
  - water accessibility (i.e. whether people are able to use physically available water supplies).
- Water intensive production processes require large volumes of water relative to other processes or industries. Water-intensive production is linked among others to industries such as agriculture; thermoelectric power; apparel; beverages; biotechnology/pharmaceuticals; electric power; forest products; high-tech (including semiconductor manufacturing, data centers); and metal/mining.
- In order to determine if a company operates a water intensive production it may, for instance:
  - rely on industry benchmark
  - conduct a water footprint or life cycle analysis
  - engage its stakeholders
  - assess water use relative to output
  - evaluate if it depends heavily on consistent water availability and is located in water-stressed regions.
