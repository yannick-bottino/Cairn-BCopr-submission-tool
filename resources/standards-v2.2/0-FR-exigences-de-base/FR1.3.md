# FR1.3 (EB 1.3) : The company complies with local and national laws and regulations.

Impact Area : Foundation Requirements (Exigences de base). Pages PDF 32-35. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**FR1** : The company meets the eligibility requirements for B Corp Certification.

**FR1.3** : The company complies with local and national laws and regulations.

## Compliance criteria

### 1.3.1

The company complies with all applicable local and national laws and regulations.

### 1.3.2

The company signs an Agreement committing to the B Corp Certification process.

## Intent

To ensure B Corps comply with applicable laws and regulations. Companies violating laws and regulations are not eligible for B Corp Certification.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[1.3.1] “Applicable laws” include all social, environmental, and governance regulations relevant to the parts of the company within scope for B Corp Certification.

[1.3.1] If national laws conflict with internationally recognized standards and conventions (including the “fundamental” International Labour Organization (ILO) Conventions [EN]) or with the B Lab Standard, the more restrictive criteria prevail, including if national legislation sets higher standards. This includes compliance with internationally recognized standards and conventions that have been adopted into national laws.

[1.3.1] Each company is responsible for its own compliance with the law. The B Lab Standard does not refer to any specific laws or regulations, because B Corps operate in many different jurisdictions, and legal requirements are always changing.

[1.3.1] The company is not eligible for certification if any evidence exists of it violating applicable laws with “severe” environmental, social, or governance impacts. “Severe” environmental, social, or governance impacts have a large scale, large scope, or low ease of remedy (see U.N.

Guiding Principles [EN]). The company may become eligible once B Lab verifies that the violation is resolved.

[1.3.2] A representative appointed by the company’s highest governing body signs an Agreement committing to the B Corp Certification process.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- Company refers to the certifying company and entities within scope of the certification.
- References to the highest governing body mean those within the company’s scope of certification. This means that a commitment to follow local and national laws and regulations by a representative from an out-of-scope parent body does not meet the sub-requirement, unless another representative at the highest governing body or executive team of the company makes an additional commitment.
