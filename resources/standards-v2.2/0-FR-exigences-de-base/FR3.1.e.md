# FR3.1.e (EB 3.1.e) : [Your products] In the past fiscal year, did the company generate at least 10% of its revenue from any of these activities? (i) Selling single-use plastic products from virgin, non-renewable materials, manufactured either by the company or for the company. (ii) Selling single-use plastic packaging from virgin, non-renewable materials, manufactured either by the company or for the company. (iii) Selling products manufactured either by the company or for the company that were packaged in single-use plastic packaging from virgin, non-renewable materials.

Impact Area : Foundation Requirements (Exigences de base). Pages PDF 79-85. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**FR3** : The company creates a risk profile and meets any additional sub-requirements.

## Intent

- Potential Impact: Negative environmental impacts related to single-use plastics

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

- The scope is the company's own operations and the production it has outsourced. The scope of packaging is primary and secondary packaging.
- "Sell" refers to selling of the finished product by the manufacturer, wholesaler, or retailer.
- “Virgin non-renewable” materials are finite materials that have not yet been used in the economy, and do not renew in short time periods (adapted from Ellen McArthur Foundation and European Sustainability Reporting Standards, 2023). Examples include iron ore mined from the ground, and plastics made without recycled content.
- The company answers this question based on its primary and secondary packaging. Tertiary packaging may be excluded.
- Primary packaging materials include: The materials that physically contain, envelop, or hold the certified product, and typically come into direct contact with the product.
- Secondary packaging materials include: Any other packaging materials contained in one sales unit as it is offered to the end user or consumer at the point of purchase and not added exclusively for shipping (e.g., a toothpaste box, outer box containing individually wrapped product units).
- Tertiary packaging materials include: Materials used exclusively for shipping the product, such as a box, pallet, or shrink or plastic wrap.

(adapted from Cradle to Cradle Certified® Product Standard, Version 4.1 [EN]).

- "Considers" means to understand how the potential impact relates to the company and decide whether it is prioritized for further action.
