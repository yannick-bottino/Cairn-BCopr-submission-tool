# FR3.1.g (EB 3.1.g) : [Your operations] In the past fiscal year, did the company's own operations have potential negative impacts on the rights or lands of Indigenous Peoples?

Impact Area : Foundation Requirements (Exigences de base). Pages PDF 94-98. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**FR3** : The company creates a risk profile and meets any additional sub-requirements.

**FR3.1.g** : [Your operations] In the past fiscal year, did the company's own operations have potential negative impacts on the rights or lands of Indigenous Peoples?

## Compliance criteria

### 3.1.g.1

[Small companies in all sectors] If Yes, in addition to the sub-requirements that already apply, the company meets the following criteria.

- a) The company meets HR2.1, 2.3, and 2.4.
- b) The company considers the potential impact (see Intent) in the human rights saliency assessment (HR2.1).

### 3.1.g.2

[Medium, Large and X Large companies in Manufacturing, Agriculture, Wholesale/Retail, and Service with significant footprint] If Yes, in addition to the sub-requirements that already apply, the company meets the following criteria.

- a) The company meets HR2.5.
- b) The company considers the potential impact (see Intent) in the human rights saliency assessment (HR2.1).

### 3.1.g.3

[Medium companies in Service with a minor footprint] If Yes, in addition to the sub-requirements that already apply, the company meets the following criteria.

- a) The company meets HR2.1, 2.3, and 2.4.
- b) The company considers the potential impact (see Intent) in the human rights saliency assessment (HR2.1).

### 3.1.g.4

[Large and X Large companies in Service with a minor footprint] If Yes, in addition to the sub-requirements that already apply, the company meets the following criteria.

- a) The company meets HR2.3, 2.4, and 2.5.
- b) The company considers the potential impact (see Intent) in the human rights saliency assessment (HR2.1).

### 3.1.g.5

[XX Large companies in all sectors] If Yes, the company considers the potential impact (see Intent) in their Human Rights Saliency Assessment (HR2.1).

## Intent

- Potential Impact: Violation of the rights of Indigenous Peoples

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

- The scope is the company's own operations
- Examples include:
  - mining
  - construction
  - industrial agriculture.
- Excludes: company practices aimed at directly protecting or promoting the rights of Indigenous Peoples.
- "Lands of Indigenous Peoples" are lands that Indigenous Peoples have legal or customary ownership of, or lands where they have usage rights. This includes lands recognized by the government and by Indigenous Peoples that may be disputed.
- "Rights of Indigenous Peoples" are the rights stated in the United Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples and in local law.
- "Considers" means to understand how the potential impact relates to the company and decide whether it is prioritized for further action.
