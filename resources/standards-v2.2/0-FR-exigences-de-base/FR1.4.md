# FR1.4 (EB 1.4) : The company shares accurate and complete information with B Lab.

Impact Area : Foundation Requirements (Exigences de base). Pages PDF 36-39. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**FR1** : The company meets the eligibility requirements for B Corp Certification.

**FR1.4** : The company shares accurate and complete information with B Lab.

## Compliance criteria

### 1.4.1

The company shares accurate and complete information by:

- a) using credible evidence and data source
- b) having a clear understanding of B Lab’s resources.

### 1.4.2

The company shares accurate and complete information at all times:

- a) in its B Impact Assessment
- b) in providing information to assurance providers authorized by B Lab
- c) in all communications with B Lab personnel.

### 1.4.3

The company signs an Agreement committing to the B Corp Certification process.

## Intent

To ensure companies receive B Corp Certification based on complete and accurate information.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[1.4.1] The company is ineligible for certification if any evidence exists of it intentionally sharing inaccurate or incomplete information. The company may become eligible again once B Lab verifies the company has addressed the issue.

[1.4.1] “Complete information” includes information required for all relevant entities or owners in the company.

[1.4.1.a] “Credible evidence and data sources” means the company:

- identifies individuals responsible for providing evidence to sub-requirements
- formally documents certain practices, such as processes and policies
- uses reliable data sources to produce calculations and analyses
- shares evidence based on current and past performance (when applicable), not on future intentions
- does not exaggerate its performance

[1.4.1.b] The company reads the B Lab Standard and any related guidance (e.g. B Lab Knowledge Base Articles) to ensure it understands each requirement

[1.4.2] Other information B Lab may require includes company information or evidence related to a formal complaint.

[1.4.3] A representative appointed by the company’s highest governing body signs an Agreement committing to the B Corp Certification process.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- Company refers to the certifying company and entities within scope of the certification.
- References to the highest governing body mean those within the company’s scope of certification. This means that a commitment to provide accurate and complete information by a representative from an out-of-scope parent body does not meet the sub-requirement, unless another representative at the highest governing body or executive team of the company makes an additional commitment.
