# FR3.1.h (EB 3.1.h) : [Your operations] In the past three years, has the company experienced any fatality or health and safety penalty?

Impact Area : Foundation Requirements (Exigences de base). Pages PDF 99-102. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**FR3** : The company creates a risk profile and meets any additional sub-requirements.

**FR3.1.h** : [Your operations] In the past three years, has the company experienced any fatality or health and safety penalty?

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

## Intent

- Potential Impact: Dangerous work, or poor health and safety practices

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

- The scope is the company's own operations
- Question applies to any worker that is or would have been within the scope of certification.
- Fatality: Death resulting from a work-related incident or exposure; in general, from an injury or an illness caused by or related to a workplace hazard. (OSHA)
- Penalties: A sanction imposed as a consequence of violating laws, regulations, or contractual obligations. It can be financial (such as fines), operational (such as restrictions or suspensions), or other forms of punishment designed to deter non-compliance and enforce rules.
- "Considers" means to understand how the potential impact relates to the company and decide whether it is prioritized for further action.
