## Intent

- Potential Impact: Contributing to forced labor

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

- The scope is the company's supply chain
- Litigation: The process of resolving disputes through the court system. It involves the filing of a lawsuit, legal proceedings, and judicial decisions.
- Penalties: A sanction imposed as a consequence of violating laws, regulations, or contractual obligations. It can be financial (such as fines), operational (such as restrictions or suspensions), or other forms of punishment designed to deter non-compliance and enforce rules.
- Public allegation: A material and credible statement that an individual or organization has engaged in illegal or unethical activities.
- In FR3, the scope of "material and credible statement" is limited to statements from:
  - news organizations
  - civil society organizations
  - governments
  - open letters or petitions with five or more named signatures.
- The scope does not include standalone social media posts. Social media posts are in scope if they are referenced in statements from news organizations; civil society organizations; governments; or open letters or petitions with five or more named signatures.
- "Forced labor" and "modern slavery" are synonymous in this context. "Forced labor" includes child labor.
- For service companies, the "supply chain" refers to suppliers of services.
