# FR1.1 (EB 1.1) : The company meets the entity qualifications to become a B Corp.

Impact Area : Foundation Requirements (Exigences de base). Pages PDF 22-24. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**FR1** : The company meets the eligibility requirements for B Corp Certification.

**FR1.1** : The company meets the entity qualifications to become a B Corp.

## Compliance criteria

### 1.1.1

The company is confirmed to be:

- a) an incorporated business entity
- b) in operation for at least 12 months
- c) generating a majority of revenue from a competitive market.

### 1.1.2

The company does not fall into any of the following categories.

- a) Business divisions, brands, or units, or businesses that are not complete and distinct from their parent or group of affiliates
- b) Franchisors who do not own a majority of global franchisee locations, or franchisors without geographic distinction from independent franchisees
- c) Non-profits or Civil Society Organizations (CSOs), foundations, or charitable entities, unless otherwise approved by B Lab
- d) Governments, government agencies, government ministries, or state-owned enterprises, unless otherwise approved by B Lab
- e) Company legally registered in regions not covered operationally by B Lab and not part of a larger group registered in a region covered by B Lab

## Intent

To ensure B Corp Certification is only available to companies. This aligns with B Lab’s mission to support and inspire companies to have positive social and environmental impacts.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[1.1.1] The following companies are eligible for B Corp Certification.

- Single business entities (i.e. the company has no parent company or subsidiaries)
- Parent companies with wholly-owned or majority-owned subsidiaries
- Subsidiaries operating as complete and distinct businesses of their parent or sibling that do not share the same name or logo, or — if they do share the same name — they must have a distinct geographic or industry qualifier
- Franchisors (owners of a brand) and majority-owned franchisees, depending on the scope and geography of branded locations

[1.1.2a, 1.1.2b] In some cases, B Corp Certification has additional requirements for related entities — such as franchisees, parent companies, or other subsidiaries in the group.

[1.2.e] For a list of regions not covered by B Lab, visit B Lab’s website [EN].
