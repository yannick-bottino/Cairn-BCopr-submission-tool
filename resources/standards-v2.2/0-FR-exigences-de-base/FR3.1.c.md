# FR3.1.c (EB 3.1.c) : [Your products] In the past fiscal year, did the company distribute a digital product that uses artificial intelligence?

Impact Area : Foundation Requirements (Exigences de base). Pages PDF 65-70. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**FR3** : The company creates a risk profile and meets any additional sub-requirements.

**FR3.1.c** : [Your products] In the past fiscal year, did the company distribute a digital product that uses artificial intelligence?

## Compliance criteria

### 3.1.c.1

[Small companies in all sectors] If Yes, in addition to the sub-requirements that already apply, the company meets the following criteria.

- a) The company meets HR2.1, 2.3, and 2.4.
- b) The company considers the potential impact (see Intent) in the human rights saliency assessment (HR2.1).

### 3.1.c.2

[Medium, Large and X Large companies in Manufacturing, Agriculture, Wholesale/Retail, and Service with significant footprint] If Yes, in addition to the sub-requirements that already apply, the company meets the following criteria.

- a) The company meets HR2.5.
- b) The company considers the potential impact (see Intent) in the human rights saliency assessment (HR2.1).

### 3.1.c.3

[Medium companies in Service with a minor footprint] If Yes, in addition to the sub-requirements that already apply, the company meets the following criteria.

- a) The company meets HR2.1, 2.3, and 2.4.
- b) The company considers the potential impact (see Intent) in the human rights saliency assessment (HR2.1).

### 3.1.c.4

[Large and X Large companies in Service with a minor footprint] If Yes, in addition to the sub-requirements that already apply, the company meets the following criteria.

- a) The company meets HR2.3, 2.4, and 2.5.
- b) The company considers the potential impact (see Intent) in the human rights saliency assessment (HR2.1).

### 3.1.c.5

[XX Large companies in all sectors] If Yes, the company considers the potential impact (see Intent) in their Human Rights Saliency Assessment (HR2.1).

## Intent

- Potential Impact: Known and unknown negative impacts to users of the digital products and society more broadly

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

- The scope is the company's own operations
- Artificial intelligence: An AI system is a machine-based system that infers, from the input it receives, how to generate outputs such as predictions, content, recommendations, or decisions that can influence physical or virtual environments. Different AI systems vary in their levels of autonomy and adaptiveness after deployment. (Adapted from OECD)
- "Distributes" refers to selling, licensing, monetizing, or otherwise providing to external users.
- "Digital product" includes both consumer products and products sold to other businesses.
- Examples of digital products that may use artificial intelligence include:
  - digital platforms and SaaS
  - consumer apps and media
  - industrial and enterprise systems
  - healthcare and biotech tools
  - creative and mixed-reality experiences.
- Excludes: physical products, including physical products that use artificial intelligence.
- "Considers" means to understand how the potential impact relates to the company and decide whether it is prioritized for further action.
