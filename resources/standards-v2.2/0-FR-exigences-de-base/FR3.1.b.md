## Intent

- Potential Impact: Negative impacts on a consumer's health from consuming or using the product

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

- The scope is the company's own operations
- Examples of products include:
  - alcohol (unless low alcohol content); low alcohol content: as per local regulations or 1.2% ABV or less
  - high free sugar content; free sugar: added sugar in any form; high sugar content: as per local regulations or when free sugars represent at least 22.5% of the product weight
  - processed red meat; red meat: all mammalian muscle meat, including beef, veal, pork, lamb, mutton, horse, and goat (WHO); processed:

salting, curing, fermentation, smoking, or other processes to enhance flavour or improve preservation (WHO)

  - lead-based paint
  - social media.
- Excludes: medicine.
- "Sell" refers to selling the finished product by the manufacturer, wholesaler, or retailer.
- "Consumer product" means for use by an individual consumer and not a business.
- "Health risks" includes both physical and mental health risks.
- "National government" refers to any government body where the company is based or where the product is consumed.
- Example of intergovernmental body: World Health Organization
- "Considers" means to understand how the potential impact relates to the company and decide whether it is prioritized for further action.
