## Intent

Potential Impact: Insecure work from being hired as an independent contractor instead of an employee

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

- The scope is the company's own operations
- The company only counts the number of independent contractors working more than 20 hours per week indefinitely or for more than six months. The company may average working hours across the period of work. If the company doesn't track the number of hours or months then it includes the independent contractor in this calculation.
- The company may count the number of independent contractors and employees based on headcount or full-time equivalent (FTE), as long as they are consistent.
- "Considers" means to understand how the Potential Impact relates to the company and decide whether it is prioritized for further action. If not prioritized, the company justifies the decision.
