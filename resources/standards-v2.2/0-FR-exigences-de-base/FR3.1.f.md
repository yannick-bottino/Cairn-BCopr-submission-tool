## Intent

- Potential Impact: Negative environmental impacts related to agricultural chemicals like fertilizers and pesticides

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

- The scope is the company's own operations
- Agricultural chemicals include the manufacturing of nitrogenous or phosphatic fertilizers, pesticides, or other agricultural chemicals and household pest control chemicals, including insecticides, fungicides, and herbicides. (adapted from SIC code 287)
- Naturally derived pesticides and fertilizers come from sources such as plants, animals, microorganisms, or minerals, and not chemically processed.
- Examples of pesticides:
  - herbicides
  - fungicides
  - insecticides.
- Examples of naturally derived pesticides and fertilizers:
  - neem oil
  - citronella
  - essential oil-based repellents
- "Sell" refers to selling of the finished product by the manufacturer, wholesaler, or retailer.
- If the company answers yes to this question it also needs to answer yes to the following question: In the past fiscal year, did the company generate at least 10% of its revenue from selling hazardous chemicals?
