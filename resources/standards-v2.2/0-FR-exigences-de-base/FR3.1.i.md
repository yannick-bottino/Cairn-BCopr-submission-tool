# FR3.1.i (EB 3.1.i) : [Your operations] In the past fiscal year, did the company manufacture or outsource the manufacturing of products made primarily from materials from extractive industries?

Impact Area : Foundation Requirements (Exigences de base). Pages PDF 103-107. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**FR3** : The company creates a risk profile and meets any additional sub-requirements.

**FR3.1.i** : [Your operations] In the past fiscal year, did the company manufacture or outsource the manufacturing of products made primarily from materials from extractive industries?

## Compliance criteria

### 3.1.i.1

[Small companies in Manufacturing, Agriculture, Wholesale/Retail, and Service with significant footprint] If Yes, in addition to the sub-requirements that already apply, the company meets the following criteria.

- a) The company meets ESC3.1 and ESC3.2.

### 3.1.i.2

[Medium companies in Manufacturing, Agriculture, Wholesale/Retail, and Service with significant footprint] If Yes, in addition to the sub-requirements that already apply, the company meets the following criteria.

- a) The company meets ESC3.1 and ESC3.2.
- b) considers the potential impact (see Intent) in their assessment of actual and potential environmental impacts (ESC1.7).

### 3.1.i.3

[Large, X Large, and XX Large companies in Manufacturing, Agriculture, Wholesale/Retail, and Service with significant footprint] If Yes, the company considers the potential impact (see Intent) in their assessment of actual and potential environmental impacts (ESC1.7).

### 3.1.i.4

[Small, Medium, Large, X Large, and XX Large companies in Service with a minor footprint] In addition to the sub-requirements that already apply, the company meets the following criteria.

- a) The company meets ESC3.1 and ESC3.2.

## Intent

- Potential Impact: Negative environmental impacts related to non-renewable resources

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

- The scope is the company's own operations and the production it has outsourced
- Extractive industries: An extractive industry can be defined as a process that involves different activities that lead to the extraction of raw materials from the earth (such as oil, metals, minerals, and aggregates), processing and utilization by consumers. (UNCTAD)
- Examples of products where extractive materials are among the primary components:
  - products made primarily of metal or plastic
  - electronics, vehicles, or batteries
  - products dependent on mined minerals or fossil fuel derivatives, like plastics or synthetic materials.
- The company may evaluate the primary component of its product based on function, volume, or value. For example:
  - functionally essential to the product’s performance or intended use (e.g., lithium in batteries)
  - used in large physical quantities, even if they are not highly valuable (e.g., sand in construction materials)
  - represent a significant portion of the product’s financial value, even if used in small amounts (e.g., gold in electronics).
- "Considers" means to understand how the potential impact relates to the company and decide whether it is prioritized for further action.
  - electronics, vehicles, or batteries
