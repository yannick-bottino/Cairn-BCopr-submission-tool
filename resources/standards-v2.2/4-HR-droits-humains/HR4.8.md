# HR4.8 (DH 4.8) : The company works with suppliers to address human rights impacts related to high-risk raw materials.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise collabore avec ses fournisseurs pour traiter les impacts liés aux matières premières à haut risque*

Impact Area : Human Rights (Droits humains). Pages PDF 706-711. Échéance : Year 5.

## Requirement

**HR4** : The company works with suppliers to achieve its human rights objectives.

**HR4.8** : The company works with suppliers to address human rights impacts related to high-risk raw materials.

## Compliance criteria

### 4.8.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 5 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company works with its prioritized suppliers to prevent and mitigate human rights impacts related to high-risk raw materials, in line with its plan (HR4.6) and its actions (HR4.7).

### 4.8.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 5 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company works with its prioritized suppliers to remediate any human rights impacts related to high-risk raw materials.

### 4.8.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 5 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The engagement process includes:

- a) an action plan and targets
- b) monitoring progress at least annually
- c) recording specific prevention and mitigation actions and their outcomes.

## Intent

To ensure the company addresses the actual and potential negative impacts of its high-risk raw materials.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[4.8.1] A “high-risk raw material” is any raw material that is known or suspected to involve severe negative human rights impacts.

[4.8.2] If the supplier is unwilling or unable to take meaningful prevention or mitigation action, the company considers:

- engaging in advocacy or other collective action to address systemic issues and enable the prevention or mitigation action [link to GACA2]
- choosing to responsibly exit its relationship with the supplier as a last resort.

[4.8.2] The following actions on their own do not meet this sub-requirement.

- Social compliance audits
- Sharing a human rights or labor code of conduct

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
