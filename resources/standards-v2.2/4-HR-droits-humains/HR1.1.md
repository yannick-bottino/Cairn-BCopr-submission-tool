# HR1.1 (DH 1.1) : The company commits publicly to respecting human rights.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise s'engage publiquement à respecter les droits humains*

Impact Area : Human Rights (Droits humains). Pages PDF 563-567. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**HR1** : The company commits publicly to respecting human rights.

**HR1.1** : The company commits publicly to respecting human rights.

## Compliance criteria

### 1.1.1

The company commits publicly to respect human rights and to each of the following:

- a) UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights
- b) The International Bill of Rights or the rights under the Universal Declaration of Human Rights
- c) The International Labour Organization’s (ILO) Declaration on Fundamental Principles and Rights at Work

## Intent

For the company to publicly commit to respecting human rights, which is an important building block of the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[1.1.1] The company may adapt the language if it operates in a country where using the term “human rights” threatens the safety of its workers.

[1.1.1] The commitment does not need to be a standalone document or webpage. It may be included with other topics. For example, the company could include both human rights and environmental commitments in one sustainability policy, as long as it met the Compliance Criteria.

[1.1.1] The ILO Declaration on Fundamental Principles and Rights at Work consists of five obligations, such as “the elimination of all forms of forced or compulsory labour”. The company may commit to the five obligations individually instead of committing to the ILO Declaration. The company may refer to “modern slavery” instead of “forced labor” as they are similar in a business context.

[1.1.1] The following types of documents do not meet this sub-requirement, unless they fulfill the Compliance Criteria.

- Ethics codes of conduct
- Supplier codes of conduct
- Modern Slavery statements
- Zero-tolerance lists

[1.1.1] The following commitments do not meet this sub-requirement.

- Commitments to the UN Sustainable Development Goals
- Commitments to diversity and inclusion
- Commitments to single issues or rights, such as ‘no child labor’

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The commitment is embedded, at minimum, at the highest level within the company. If the company references a commitment belonging to an out-of-scope parent, it records evidence of roll-out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms to support the commitment.
