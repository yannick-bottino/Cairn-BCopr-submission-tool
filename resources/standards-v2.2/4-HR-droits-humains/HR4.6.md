# HR4.6 (DH 4.6) : The company has a time-bound plan to trace the origin and potential human rights impacts of its high-risk raw materials.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise dispose d'un plan assorti d'un calendrier pour retracer origine et impacts de matières premières à haut risque*

Impact Area : Human Rights (Droits humains). Pages PDF 696-700. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**HR4** : The company works with suppliers to achieve its human rights objectives.

**HR4.6** : The company has a time-bound plan to trace the origin and potential human rights impacts of its high-risk raw materials.

## Compliance criteria

### 4.6.1

The company identifies its high-risk raw materials.

### 4.6.2

The company either:

- a) knows the origin country and potential human rights impacts of its high-risk raw materials
- b) has a time-bound plan to trace and fully map its supply chain across all supplier tiers and for all high-risk raw materials.

### 4.6.3

The company documents the proportion of high-risk raw materials that it can trace to their origin.

## Intent

For the company to map its supply chain and mitigate the actual and potential negative impacts of its high-risk raw materials.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[4.6.1] A “high-risk raw material” is any raw material that is known or suspected to involve severe negative human rights impacts.

[4.6.1] The company identifies which of its raw materials are “high-risk”.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
