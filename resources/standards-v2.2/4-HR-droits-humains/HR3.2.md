# HR3.2 (DH 3.2) : The company prevents, mitigates, and remediates actual and potential negative human rights impacts.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise prévient, atténue et répare les impacts négatifs réels et potentiels*

Impact Area : Human Rights (Droits humains). Pages PDF 621-627. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**HR3** : The company prevents, mitigates, and remediates actual and potential negative human rights impacts.

**HR3.2** : The company prevents, mitigates, and remediates actual and potential negative human rights impacts.

## Compliance criteria

### 3.2.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company takes action to prevent and mitigate actual and potential negative human rights impacts.

### 3.2.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company takes action to remediate any actual negative human rights impacts.

### 3.2.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company considers:

- a) its type of connection to the impact
- b) the views of affected stakeholders.

## Intent

To ensure the company’s awareness of potential negative impacts leads to prevention and mitigation actions. Prevention and mitigation is proactive, meaning it addresses a potential impact before it becomes an actual impact.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[3.2.3 b] The company may engage with stakeholder representatives — rather than directly to stakeholders  — where it is needed or more appropriate, and supported by stakeholders. For example, the company may speak to a union or civil society organization that represents the interests of affected stakeholders.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The actions take place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
