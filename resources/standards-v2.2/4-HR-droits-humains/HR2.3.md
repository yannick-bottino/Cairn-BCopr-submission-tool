# HR2.3 (DH 2.3) : The company has a strategy to address its salient human rights issues.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise dispose d'une stratégie pour traiter ses enjeux majeurs en matière de droits humains*

Impact Area : Human Rights (Droits humains). Pages PDF 589-594. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**HR2** : The company knows its salient human rights issues and has a strategy to address them.

**HR2.3** : The company has a strategy to address its salient human rights issues.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.3.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company has a strategy to address its salient human rights issues.

### 2.3.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The strategy was adopted or reviewed in the last twelve months.

### 2.3.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The strategy is approved by the highest governing body.

### 2.3.4

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The strategy accounts for the company’s type of connection with each salient human rights issue.

### 2.3.5

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The strategy, or a related implementation plan, includes:

- a) targets for the next three years at minimum
- b) indicators
- c) clearly defined roles and responsibilities
- d) engagement with stakeholders.

## Intent

For the company to create a strategy from its assessment of salient human rights issues.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.3.1] The strategy sets out how the company intends to address each of its salient human rights issues. More specifically, it describes how the company will:

- assess the potential negative human rights impacts of working with potential clients and projects, if applicable [link to HR3.3]
- assess the potential negative human rights impacts of investments, if applicable [link to HR3.4]
- collect, prioritize, and escalate information on actual and potential negative human rights impacts [links to HR3.1]
- use a Human Rights Impact Assessment, if applicable [links to HR3.5]
- use inputs from stakeholders [links to PSG2]
- prevent, mitigate, and remediate actual and potential negative human rights impacts [link to HR3.2]
- evaluate the effectiveness of the strategy [link to HR2.4]
- work with suppliers to achieve human rights objectives and trace raw materials in the supply chain to their origins [link to HR4].

[2.3.1] The company may adapt the language if it operates in a country where using the term “human rights” threatens the safety of its workers.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The strategy is embedded, at minimum, at the highest governance level within the company. If the company references a strategy belonging to an out-of-scope parent, it records evidence of roll-out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms to support the strategy.
- References to the highest governing body or executive team mean those within the company’s scope of certification. This means that approval by an out-of-scope parent body does not meet the sub-requirement, unless the company has additional approval at the highest governing body or executive team within its scope of certification.
