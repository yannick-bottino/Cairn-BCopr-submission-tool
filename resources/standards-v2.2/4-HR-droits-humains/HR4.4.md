# HR4.4 (DH 4.4) : The company works with suppliers to prevent or mitigate the most salient human rights issues.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise collabore avec ses fournisseurs pour prévenir ou atténuer les enjeux majeurs*

Impact Area : Human Rights (Droits humains). Pages PDF 684-690. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**HR4** : The company works with suppliers to achieve its human rights objectives.

**HR4.4** : The company works with suppliers to prevent or mitigate the most salient human rights issues.

## Compliance criteria

### 4.4.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company:

- a) identifies prioritized suppliers related to its salient human rights issues (HR2.1)
- b) records the criteria it used to prioritize suppliers.

### 4.4.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company works with its prioritized suppliers to prevent or mitigate their most material human rights impacts by

- a) agreeing on targets with each prioritized supplier
- b) monitoring progress at least annually

### 4.4.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

For Years 3 and 5, the company records specific prevention and mitigation actions and their outcomes.

## Intent

To ensure the company’s awareness of potential negative impacts in the supply chain leads to prevention and mitigation actions. Prevention and mitigation is proactive, meaning it addresses a potential impact before it becomes an actual impact.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[4.4.1] This sub-requirement applies to suppliers at all tiers.

[4.4.2] If the supplier is unwilling or unable to take meaningful prevention or mitigation action, the company considers:

- engaging in advocacy or other collective action to address systemic issues and enable the prevention or mitigation action [link to GACA2]
- choosing to responsibly exit its relationship with the supplier as a last resort.

[4.4.2] The following actions on their own do not meet this sub-requirement.

- Social compliance audits
- Sharing a human rights or labor code of conduct

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
