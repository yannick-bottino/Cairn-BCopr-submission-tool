# HR3.1 (DH 3.1) : The company has a process to collect, prioritize, and escalate information on actual and potential negative human rights impacts.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise dispose d'un processus pour collecter, hiérarchiser et faire remonter les impacts négatifs*

Impact Area : Human Rights (Droits humains). Pages PDF 614-620. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**HR3** : The company prevents, mitigates, and remediates actual and potential negative human rights impacts.

**HR3.1** : The company has a process to collect, prioritize, and escalate information on actual and potential negative human rights impacts.

## Compliance criteria

### 3.1.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company has a process to collect, prioritize, and escalate information on actual and potential negative human rights impacts.

### 3.1.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company has clear roles and responsibilities related to this process.

### 3.1.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

In the process, impacts are prioritized and escalated based on their severity and likelihood.

### 3.1.4

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The process also sets out:

- a) when and how to seek additional information about an actual or potential negative impact, and how to involve stakeholders, including affected stakeholders
- b) what kinds of internal changes at the company will prompt it to proactively assess potential negative impacts.

## Intent

To ensure the company adequately processes information about human rights issues so it can respond to them appropriately. Processing information internally is separate from the company’s response to that information.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[3.1.1] “Escalate” refers to sharing information with relevant decision-makers or those with a responsibility over an issue. This may include sharing information upwards within the company hierarchy, such as to the executive team or highest governing body.

[3.1.3] The company prioritizes and escalates specific issues based on severity and likelihood. Severity should outweigh likelihood, so that highly severe impacts — even if they are unlikely — are still prioritized.

- Severity refers to one or more of the following.
  - Scale — how serious would the impact be? A bigger scale is more severe.
  - Scope — how many people would be impacted? A bigger scope is more severe.
  - Ease of remediation — how easy would it be to remediate the potential negative impact? A higher difficulty of remediation is more severe. For example, it is easier to remediate underpaid wages than someone contracting an incurable disease due to poor health and safety conditions at work. Note, this does not mean less severe human rights impacts should not be remedied.
- Likelihood considers how likely the impact is, and how frequently it might occur.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The process is embedded, at minimum, at the highest level within the company. If the company references a process belonging to an out-of-scope parent, it records evidence of roll-out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms to support the procedure.
