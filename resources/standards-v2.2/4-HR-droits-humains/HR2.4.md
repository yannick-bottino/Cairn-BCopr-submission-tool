# HR2.4 (DH 2.4) : The company makes progress on its human rights strategy and evaluates its effectiveness.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise met en œuvre sa stratégie en matière de droits humains et évalue son efficacité*

Impact Area : Human Rights (Droits humains). Pages PDF 595-599. Échéance : Year 5.

## Requirement

**HR2** : The company knows its salient human rights issues and has a strategy to address them.

**HR2.4** : The company makes progress on its human rights strategy and evaluates its effectiveness.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.4.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 5 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company makes progress against its targets and indicators (measured at an interval of no more than three years), in line with its human rights strategy (HR2.3).

### 2.4.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 5 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company evaluates the effectiveness of its human rights strategy (HR2.3).

### 2.4.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 5 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

Where the strategy is ineffective, the company records:

- a) what lessons it has learned
- b) what it plans to do differently
- c) how it has updated the strategy.

### 2.4.4

*Échéance du critère : Year 5 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The evaluation results are shared with the highest governing body or executive team.

## Intent

For the company to put its human rights strategy into practice and ensure it works as intended. The intent of the strategy is for the company to evaluate, learn, and continuously improve. Completely eliminating all actual and potential negative impacts is impossible and therefore not the intent.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
- References to the highest governing body or executive team mean those within the certifying company’s scope of certification. This means that sharing the results with an out-of-scope parent body does not meet the sub-requirement, unless the certifying company has also shared the results with the highest governing body or executive team within its scope of certification.
