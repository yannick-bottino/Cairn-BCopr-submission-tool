# HR4.7 (DH 4.7) : The company traces more high-risk raw materials to their origin.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise trace un nombre croissant de matières premières à haut risque jusqu'à leur origine*

Impact Area : Human Rights (Droits humains). Pages PDF 701-705. Échéance : Year 5.

## Requirement

**HR4** : The company works with suppliers to achieve its human rights objectives.

**HR4.7** : The company traces more high-risk raw materials to their origin.

## Compliance criteria

### 4.7.1

The company increases the proportion of high-risk raw materials that it ckritische Rohstoffan trace to their origin according to their plan (HR4.6).

## Intent

To ensure the company makes progress toward mapping the high-risk raw materials in its supply chain.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[4.7.1] A “high-risk raw material” is any raw material that is known or suspected to involve severe negative human rights impacts.

[4.7.1] The company identifies which of its raw materials are “high-risk”.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
