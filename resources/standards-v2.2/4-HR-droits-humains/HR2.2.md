# HR2.2 (DH 2.2) : The company publicly shares its salient human rights issues.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise partage publiquement ses enjeux majeurs en matière de droits humains*

Impact Area : Human Rights (Droits humains). Pages PDF 585-588. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**HR2** : The company knows its salient human rights issues and has a strategy to address them.

**HR2.2** : The company publicly shares its salient human rights issues.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.2.1

The company publicly shares its salient human rights issues (HR2.1).

### 2.2.2

The company publicly shares the methodology it used in its assessment of salient human rights issues.

## Intent

To increase public accountability and enable companies to compare their salient human rights issues.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The reporting scope covers the company. If the company references a corporate group report that includes out-of-scope entities, either:
  - the performance of the company is clearly identifiable within the group report
  - the company publicly shares its own report on its webpage.
