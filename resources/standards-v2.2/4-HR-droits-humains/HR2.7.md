# HR2.7 (DH 2.7) : Workers in relevant roles receive the guidance they need to implement the policies and procedures related to human rights.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : Les collaborateurs occupant des postes concernés reçoivent les orientations nécessaires pour mettre en œuvre les politiques et procédures*

Impact Area : Human Rights (Droits humains). Pages PDF 609-613. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**HR2** : The company knows its salient human rights issues and has a strategy to address them.

**HR2.7** : Workers in relevant roles receive the guidance they need to implement the policies and procedures related to human rights.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.7.1

Workers in relevant roles receive training, guidance documents, or tools to help implement the policies and procedures related to human rights.

### 2.7.2

The guidance is tailored for the relevant role.

## Intent

To ensure the right people are prepared to implement the policies and procedures related to human rights.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.7.1] “The policies and procedures related to human rights” are those related to each salient human rights issue (HR2.6)

[2.7.1] Relevant roles can be at any level within a company. They may range from members of the highest governing body to workers who interact directly with vulnerable people.

[2.7.2] Workers in relevant roles are given information tailored to their role. Generic training about the company’s salient human rights issues is not sufficient.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
