# HR4.10 (DH 4.10) : The company refers to living wage in its service procurement processes.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise fait référence au salaire de subsistance dans ses processus d'achat de services*

Impact Area : Human Rights (Droits humains). Pages PDF 724-734. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**HR4** : The company works with suppliers to achieve its human rights objectives.

**HR4.10** : The company refers to living wage in its service procurement processes.

## Compliance criteria

### 4.10.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 3 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company refers to living wages in its service procurement documents, process, or conversations for any service with a potential living wage gap.

## Intent

To ensure the company considers living wages or collective bargaining when procuring services.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[4.10.1] The “potential living wage gaps” are those identified in the assessment (HR4.9).

[4.10.1] The company does not need to refer to living wage where there is:

- no potential living wage gap
- no free living wage estimate data
- a collective bargaining agreement.

[4.10.1] This sub-requirement does not restrict which living wage estimates can be used (unlike FW2.8).

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
