# HR2.6 (DH 2.6) : The company’s policies and procedures address its salient human rights issues.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : Les politiques et procédures de l'entreprise couvrent ses enjeux majeurs en matière de droits humains*

Impact Area : Human Rights (Droits humains). Pages PDF 604-608. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**HR2** : The company knows its salient human rights issues and has a strategy to address them.

**HR2.6** : The company’s policies and procedures address its salient human rights issues.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.6.1

The company explicitly addresses each salient human rights issue with a policy or procedure.

### 2.6.2

The scope of each policy and procedure matches the nature of the company’s salient issues.

## Intent

To ensure the company’s policies and procedures address all salient human rights issues. For example, if forced labor is a salient human rights issue, the company has a policy on forced labor.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.6.2] Matching the “scope” of policies and procedures to salient issues means ensuring they apply where the issues are relevant (e.g. to the company’s own workers versus workers in the supply chain, or to operations in one territory versus the company’s global operations). For example, a policy to close the gender pay gap among the company’s own workers would not address discrimination among workers in the supply chain, because the scope is different.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The policies and procedures are embedded, at minimum, at the highest level within the company. If the company references a policy or procedure belonging to an out-of-scope parent, it records evidence of roll-out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms to support the policy or procedure.
