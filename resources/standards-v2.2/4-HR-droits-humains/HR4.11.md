# HR4.11 (DH 4.11) : The company has a plan to address living wage, living income or collective bargaining in its supply chain.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise dispose d'un plan pour aborder le salaire de subsistance, revenu de subsistance ou négociation collective*

Impact Area : Human Rights (Droits humains). Pages PDF 735-748. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**HR4** : The company works with suppliers to achieve its human rights objectives.

**HR4.11** : The company has a plan to address living wage, living income or collective bargaining in its supply chain.

## Compliance criteria

### 4.11.1

The company’s plan relates to one or more of the following:

- a) living wage
- b) living income
- c) collective bargaining

### 4.11.2

The company’s plan includes, at minimum:

- a) an overview of known and unknown living wage or living income gaps
- b) an overview of priority area
- c) targets for the next three years
- d) engagement with stakeholders, including suppliers.

### 4.11.3

The plan was reviewed or updated in the last twelve months.

### 4.11.4

The plan is approved by the company’s executive team or highest governing body.

### 4.11.5

The company has publicly shared an update.

### 4.11.6

The public update includes, at minimum:

- a) actions the company has taken in priority areas
- b) achievements and challenges.

## Intent

To increase visibility of potential living wage and living income gaps in the company’s supply chain.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[4.11.1] The company’s plan may refer to “freedom of association” or “social dialogue” instead of “collective bargaining”.

[4.11.2 b] The company can identify its “priority areas” by country, supplier, material, or product group.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The plan is embedded, at minimum, at the highest level within the company. If the company references a plan belonging to an out-of-scope parent, it records evidence of roll-out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms to support the plan.
- References to the highest governing body or executive team mean those within the company’s scope of certification. This means that approval by an out-of-scope parent body does not meet the sub-requirement, unless the company has additional approval at the highest governing body or executive team within its scope of certification.
