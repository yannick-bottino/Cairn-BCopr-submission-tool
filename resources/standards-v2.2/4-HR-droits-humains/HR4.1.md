# HR4.1 (DH 4.1) : The company addresses limits on its ability to engage and monitor suppliers.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise identifie les limites de sa capacité à engager et suivre ses fournisseurs*

Impact Area : Human Rights (Droits humains). Pages PDF 663-668. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**HR4** : The company works with suppliers to achieve its human rights objectives.

**HR4.1** : The company addresses limits on its ability to engage and monitor suppliers.

## Compliance criteria

### 4.1.1

The company records information about potential negative human rights impacts related to:

- a) source countries
- b) specific raw materials.

### 4.1.2

The company:

- a) identifies limits on its ability to monitor suppliers in source countries
- b) has mitigation actions in place.

### 4.1.3

If the company uses social compliance audits or certifications to monitor suppliers, it:

- a) identifies any limits on its approach
- b) addresses any limits.

### 4.1.4

When selecting source countries and raw materials, the company:

- a) considers potential negative human rights impacts
- b) assesses and addresses limits on supplier monitoring.

## Intent

- For the company to consider the negative human rights impacts related to its source countries and raw materials. This helps prevent a situation where the company works with any source country or raw material by default, and treats human rights as an afterthought.
- To avoid companies basing their supplier engagement and monitoring solely on social compliance audits. Used in isolation, social compliance audits and certification can give a false sense of clarity and assurance. Through this and other sub-requirements in the B Lab Standard, companies will take an approach that goes beyond compliance. See Recommendations for more information about the shortcomings of social compliance audits.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it records evidence of:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
