# HR3.5 (DH 3.5) : The company carries out a Human Rights Impact Assessment.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise réalise une évaluation d'impact sur les droits humains*

Impact Area : Human Rights (Droits humains). Pages PDF 651-655. Échéance : Year 5.

## Requirement

**HR3** : The company prevents, mitigates, and remediates actual and potential negative human rights impacts.

**HR3.5** : The company carries out a Human Rights Impact Assessment.

## Compliance criteria

### 3.5.1

The company has carried out a Human Rights Impact Assessment in the last five years.

### 3.5.2

The assessment:

- a) focuses on a high-risk context
- b) is based on meaningful engagement with stakeholders, including affected stakeholders
- c) assesses actual and potential negative impacts
- d) reflects the company’s type of connection
- e) is carried out by people or an organization with relevant expertise.

### 3.5.3

The company publicly shares a summary of the assessment results at minimum.

## Intent

To ensure the company assesses human rights impacts in the most high-risk contexts. The Human Rights Impact Assessment is intended to more closely examine a high-risk context than the assessment of salient human rights issues.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[3.5.1] A social compliance audit does not count as a Human Rights Impact Assessment.

[3.5.2 a] The assessment focuses on either:

- an existing salient issue
- a new issue that is likely to become salient.

[3.5.2 a] Focusing on a “high-risk context” means targeting the assessment at a specific issue, place, or practice — for example:

- poverty wages in Vietnam
- abuse and harassment by senior leadership
- multiple human rights impacts from a construction project displacing a community.

[3.5.2 b] “Meaningful engagement with stakeholders” involves open discussion.

[3.5.2 e] “Relevant expertise” means the people or organization have:

- experience carrying out Human Rights Impact Assessments
- expertise in the impact areas to be assessed (e.g. forced labor)
- expertise in the relevant geographical or cultural context.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The assessment covers the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate assessment, it records evidence of:
  - how the assessment considered its stakeholders and operational impacts
  - how the assessment outcome is relevant to its context.
