# CA3.4 (AC 3.4) : The company makes progress on its climate transition plan and evaluates its effectiveness.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise réalise des progrès sur son plan de transition climatique et évalue son efficacité*

Impact Area : Climate Action (Action climatique). Pages PDF 832-838. Échéance : Year 5.

## Requirement

**CA3** : The company implements and makes progress on its climate action plan.

**CA3.4** : The company makes progress on its climate transition plan and evaluates its effectiveness.

## Compliance criteria

### 3.4.1

The company records its progress toward its near-term and net zero targets, both:

- a) in metric tonnes of CO2 equivalent
- b) as a percentage of the emissions in its baseline year.

### 3.4.2

The company:

- a) takes actions aligned with the latest version of its climate action plan
- b) continues to set near-term targets every 60 months.

### 3.4.3

Where the climate transition plan was ineffective, the company evaluates the plan and records:

- a) what lessons it has learned
- b) what it plans to do differently
- c) how it has updated the transition plan.

### 3.4.4

If the company has reached its net zero target, it:

- a) ensures permanent removals of any residual emissions
- b) maintains the removals and emissions reductions it has achieved
- c) records the amount of greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions removals or storage in its operations or value chain (in metric tonnes of CO2 equivalent).

## Intent

For the company to make progress on its climate action plan, and ensure its actions lead to the intended results.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[3.4.1] When recording its progress in metric tons of CO2 equivalent, the company identifies the change in emissions by subtracting its baseline year emissions from the emissions in the reporting year.

[3.4.1] When recording  its progress as a percentage of the emissions in its baseline year, the company applies the following formula.

- Progress (%) = (change in emissions / baseline year emissions) X 100.

[3.4.1] Evaluating the effectiveness of the company’s climate transition plan goes beyond assessing its targets and key indicators.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
