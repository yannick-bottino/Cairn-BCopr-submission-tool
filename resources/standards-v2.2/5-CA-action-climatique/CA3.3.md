# CA3.3 (AC 3.3) : The company makes progress on its climate action plan and evaluates its effectiveness.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise fait progresser son plan d'action climatique et évalue son efficacité*

Impact Area : Climate Action (Action climatique). Pages PDF 827-831. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**CA3** : The company implements and makes progress on its climate action plan.

**CA3.3** : The company makes progress on its climate action plan and evaluates its effectiveness.

## Compliance criteria

### 3.3.1

The company records its progress against its climate action plan.

### 3.3.2

The company:

- a) takes actions aligned with the latest version of its climate action plan
- b) reviews and updates its targets where necessary.

### 3.3.3

Where the climate action plan was ineffective, the company evaluates the plan and records:

- a) what lessons it has learned
- b) what it plans to do differently
- c) how it has updated its action plan.

## Intent

For the company to make progress on its climate action plan, and ensure its actions lead to the intended results. The intent of the plan is for the company to evaluate, learn, and continuously improve.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
