# CA3.5 (AC 3.5) : The company takes action for a just transition.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise met en place des actions pour une transition juste*

Impact Area : Climate Action (Action climatique). Pages PDF 839-843. Échéance : Year 5.

## Requirement

**CA3** : The company implements and makes progress on its climate action plan.

**CA3.5** : The company takes action for a just transition.

## Compliance criteria

### 3.5.1

Following consultation with workers and stakeholders on its climate transition plan (in CA2.4), the company records its actions to support a just transition.

## Intent

For the company to take action toward a just transition, and monitor its progress to ensure its efforts have been effective.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[3.5.1] A just transition is a continuous process. As the company engages with stakeholders on its climate transition plan, it ensures the outcomes of these discussions lead to practical steps, and tracks progress toward them. [link to CA2.4].

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:
  - roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
  - how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
