# GACA3.1 (APAC 3.1) : The company has a public policy on responsible taxes.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise dispose d'une politique en matière de fiscalité responsable et la rend publique*

Impact Area : Government Affairs & Collective Action (Affaires publiques & action collective). Pages PDF 1237-1241. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**GACA3** : The company takes a responsible and transparent approach to taxes.

**GACA3.1** : The company has a public policy on responsible taxes.

## Compliance criteria

### 3.1.1

The company’s tax policy states its approach to responsible taxes, including:

- a) how its approach to tax is linked to the company’s business and sustainable development strategies
- b) its approach to regulatory compliance
- c) which governance body or executive-level position within the company will formally review and approve the policy, and how frequently.

### 3.1.2

The company’s tax policy states its governance, control, and risk management practices, including:

- a) which governance body or executive-level position within the company is accountable for enforcing the policy
- b) how the policy is embedded within the company, including how the company puts it into practice
- c) how the company identifies, manages, and monitors risks of non-compliance
- d) how the company evaluates compliance with the governance and control framework
- e) how stakeholders can raise concerns about the company’s business conduct and tax practices, including reference to the company’s grievance procedure [link to PSG3]
- f) a description of the company's assurance process for public tax reporting, including, if applicable, a link or reference to any external assurance reports or statements.

### 3.1.3

The company’s tax policy states how it engages with stakeholders and manages tax-related concerns, including:

- a) its approach to engaging with tax authorities
- b) its approach to public policy advocacy related to tax
- c) how it collects and considers stakeholders’ views and concerns, including from external stakeholders.

### 3.1.4

The company’s tax policy is:

- a) approved by the highest governing body
- b) published on the company’s webpage and made accessible to all stakeholders.

## Intent

To ensure companies are responsible and transparent about the taxes they pay. Taxes are vital in achieving the U.N. Sustainable Development Goals, and a key way for companies to contribute to their local economies. By committing to a responsible tax policy, and publicly reporting their tax payments, companies reinforce their contribution to tackling social and environmental challenges.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[3.1.1] The following do not count towards meeting this Compliance Criteria.

- Company documents that only focus on legal or regulatory compliance
- Tax references in a broader company policy that does not directly address responsible taxes (e.g. a Code of Ethics that references bribery and corruption)

[3.1.2.d] The governance and control framework is described in Compliance Criteria 3.1.2.a-3.1.2.c.

[3.1.4] The tax policy can be a standalone document, or integrated into another document. Either form is acceptable, as long as the policy meets the Compliance Criteria.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- The policy is embedded, at minimum, at the highest governance level within the company. If the company references a policy belonging to an out-of-scope parent, it records evidence of roll-out, enforcement and accountability mechanisms to support the policy.
- References to the highest governing body or executive team mean those within the company’s scope of certification. This means that approval by an out-of-scope parent body does not meet the sub-requirement, unless the company has additional approval at the highest governing body or executive team within its scope of certification.
