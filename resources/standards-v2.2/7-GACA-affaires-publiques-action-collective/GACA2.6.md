# GACA2.6 (APAC 2.6) : The company includes multi-stakeholder collaboration or public policy advocacy as one of its collective actions.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise inclut les initiatives multipartites ou le plaidoyer en faveur de politiques publiques*

Impact Area : Government Affairs & Collective Action (Affaires publiques & action collective). Pages PDF 1234-1236. Échéance : Year 3.

## Requirement

**GACA2** : The company works collaboratively to advance collective social or environmental impacts.

**GACA2.6** : The company includes multi-stakeholder collaboration or public policy advocacy as one of its collective actions.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.6.1

The company includes one of the following in its collective actions.

- a) GACA2.3c The company collaborates with multiple stakeholders to advance social or environmental impacts with clear contribution.
- b) GACA2.3d The company promotes public policy to advance social or environmental impacts with clear contribution.

## Intent

To require large companies to engage in collective actions with the biggest impact. Larger companies are better-resourced to support mul-ti-stakeholder collaboration, and they often have greater ability to advocate for systemic social and environmental change. This sub-requirement ensures that the biggest B Corps use their size and power effectively.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.6.1] See the compliance criteria under GACA2.3c and GACA2.3d for details.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:

- roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
- how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
