# GACA2.1c (APAC 2.1c) : The company collaborates with multiple stakeholders to advance social or environmental impacts.

Impact Area : Government Affairs & Collective Action (Affaires publiques & action collective). Pages PDF 1190-1194. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**GACA2** : The company works collaboratively to advance collective social or environmental impacts.

**GACA2.1c** : The company collaborates with multiple stakeholders to advance social or environmental impacts.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.1c.1

The multi-stakeholder collaboration:

- a) has a clear purpose and aims to deliver specific, measurable impacts collaboratively.
- b) aims to create a positive impact on society or the environment.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.1c.1] The multi-stakeholder collaboration includes stakeholder(s) that are external to the company.

[2.1c.1] When engaging in multi-stakeholder collaboration, particularly at the industry level, the company follows any applicable local and national antitrust laws.

[2.1c.1.a] If the company is an observer (meaning it attends sessions without contributing) or a signatory of the project, it meets this Compliance Criteria by integrating the outputs or learnings of the project into its operations.

[2.1c.1.b] To determine whether the multi-stakeholder collaboration contributes to a positive impact on society or the environment, the company consults either:

- the outcome sections of the B Lab Standard
- the U.N. Sustainable Development Goals or similar frameworks.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:

- roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
- how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
