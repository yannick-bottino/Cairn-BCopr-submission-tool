# GACA2.1 (APAC 2.1) : The company takes part in collective action to advance social or environmental impacts.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise prend part à des actions collectives visant à promouvoir des impacts sociaux ou environnementaux positifs*

Impact Area : Government Affairs & Collective Action (Affaires publiques & action collective). Pages PDF 1176-1178. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**GACA2** : The company works collaboratively to advance collective social or environmental impacts.

**GACA2.1** : The company takes part in collective action to advance social or environmental impacts.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.1.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company takes part in collective action from the five options in this Impact Topic.

- a) GACA2.1a The company mentors others in its industry, profession, or value chain to advance their social or environmental impacts.
- b) GACA2.1b The company contributes to external research to advance social or environmental impacts.
- c) GACA2.1c The company collaborates with multiple stakeholders to advance social or environmental impacts.
- d) GACA2.1d The company promotes public policy to advance social or environmental impacts
- e) GACA2.1e The company uses thought leadership to drive systemic change towards an equitable, inclusive, and regenerative economy.

### 2.1.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company takes part in at least one collective action.

## Intent

To encourage companies to have positive social and environmental impacts and act beyond their own interests. Companies have a key role to play in collective solutions to systemic social and environmental challenges. Embracing collective action is an essential part of being a B Corp.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.1.1] Any collective action initiative the company takes to comply with the B Lab Standard may count toward meeting this sub-requirement.

[2.1.1] See the Compliance Criteria under the collective action sub-requirements for details.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

- This sub-requirement applies equally, whether the company is:
  - a parent company
  - an independent company.
- Companies that are part of a corporate family in a group certification may meet this sub-requirement through actions at the level of:
  - the parent or group
  - a global industry business unit
  - a geographic market or local subsidiary
  - a combination of the above.
