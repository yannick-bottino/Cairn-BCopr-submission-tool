# GACA2.3b (APAC 2.3b) : The company contributes to external research to advance social or environmental impacts.

Impact Area : Government Affairs & Collective Action (Affaires publiques & action collective). Pages PDF 1217-1220. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**GACA2** : The company works collaboratively to advance collective social or environmental impacts.

**GACA2.3b** : The company contributes to external research to advance social or environmental impacts.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.3b.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The external research aims to create a positive impact on society or the environment.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 2.3b.1 La recherche externe vise à créer un impact positif sur la société ou l'environnement.

### 2.3b.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company contributes to research through:

- a) financial contributions
- b) in-kind contributions
- c) data sharing.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 2.3b.2 L'entreprise contribue à la recherche par :
a) Des contributions financières.
b) Des contributions en nature.
c) Le partage de données.

### 2.3b.3

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company follows up on the output of the research within twelve months.

*Traduction FR (à relire, compilation tierce) :* 2.3b.3 L'entreprise assure le suivi des résultats de la recherche dans un délai de douze mois.

## Intent

None

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.3b.1] To determine whether the research contributes to a positive impact on society or the environment, the company consults either:

- the outcome sections of the B Lab Standard
- the U.N. Sustainable Development Goals or similar frameworks.

[2.3b.1] “External research” means the research is not driven by the company’s own research and development team.

[2.3b.1] The research relates to the company’s sector or industry.

[2.3b.2.a] If the company makes financial or in-kind contributions, their value is at least 0.2% of the company’s revenue in a fiscal year.

[2.3b.2.c] If the company shares data, the data:

- is comprehensive (meaning it covers the full scope of the company or an issue)
- is not freely available
- meets the specific needs of the research project.

[2.3b.3] The company follows up on the output of the research to ensure the researcher is working toward the intended outcome. It is acceptable for a research output to be unavailable in time for Year 0, as long as the company follows up for Years 3 and 5.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:

- roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
- how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
