# GACA2.3a (APAC 2.3a) : The company mentors others in its industry, profession, or value chain to advance their social or environmental impacts.

Impact Area : Government Affairs & Collective Action (Affaires publiques & action collective). Pages PDF 1213-1216. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**GACA2** : The company works collaboratively to advance collective social or environmental impacts.

**GACA2.3a** : The company mentors others in its industry, profession, or value chain to advance their social or environmental impacts.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.3a.1

The company’s mentoring has:

- a) a clear objective aligned with advancing the mentee’s social or environmental impact
- b) a clear positive outcome that the mentee confirms
- c) a systematic approach targeting a specific group of mentees.

## Intent

None

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.3a.1] The mentee is a stakeholder from outside the company with relevance to the company’s industry, profession, or value chain.

[2.3a.1] The mentoring suits the needs of the mentee.

[2.3a.1] The company does not receive financial compensation for the mentoring.

[2.3a.1.a] To determine whether the mentoring contributes to a positive impact on society or the environment, the company consults either:

- the outcome sections of the B Lab Standard
- the U.N. Sustainable Development Goals or similar frameworks.

[2.3a.1.c] A “systematic approach” to mentoring means having a defined program with a timeline, milestones, and deliverables.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:

- roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
- how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
