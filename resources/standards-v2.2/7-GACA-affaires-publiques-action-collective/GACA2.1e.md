# GACA2.1e (APAC 2.1e) : The company uses thought leadership to drive systemic change towards an equitable, inclusive, and regenerative economy.

Impact Area : Government Affairs & Collective Action (Affaires publiques & action collective). Pages PDF 1200-1203. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**GACA2** : The company works collaboratively to advance collective social or environmental impacts.

**GACA2.1e** : The company uses thought leadership to drive systemic change towards an equitable, inclusive, and regenerative economy.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.1e.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The thought leadership aims to create a positive impact on society or the environment.

## Intent

None

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.1e.1] To determine whether the thought leadership contributes to a positive impact on society or the environment, the company consults either:

- the outcome sections of the B Lab Standard
- the U.N. Sustainable Development Goals or similar frameworks.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:

- roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
- how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
