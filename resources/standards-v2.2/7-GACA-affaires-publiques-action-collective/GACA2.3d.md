# GACA2.3d (APAC 2.3d) : The company promotes public policy to advance social or environmental impacts with clear contribution.

Impact Area : Government Affairs & Collective Action (Affaires publiques & action collective). Pages PDF 1226-1229. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**GACA2** : The company works collaboratively to advance collective social or environmental impacts.

**GACA2.3d** : The company promotes public policy to advance social or environmental impacts with clear contribution.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.3d.1

The advocacy work aims to create a positive impact on society or the environment.

### 2.3d.2

The company has one or more resources to support its public policy advocacy work:

- a) financial contributions
- b) in-kind contribution
- c) active staff time.

### 2.3d.3

The company makes a clear contribution to public policy advocacy.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.3d.1] To determine whether its advocacy contributes to a positive impact on society or the environment, the company consults either:

- the outcome sections of the B Lab Standard
- the U.N. Sustainable Development Goals or similar frameworks.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:

- roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
- how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
