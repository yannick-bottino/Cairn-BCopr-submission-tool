# GACA2.3c (APAC 2.3c) : The company collaborates with multiple stakeholders to advance social or environmental impacts with clear contribution.

Impact Area : Government Affairs & Collective Action (Affaires publiques & action collective). Pages PDF 1221-1225. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**GACA2** : The company works collaboratively to advance collective social or environmental impacts.

**GACA2.3c** : The company collaborates with multiple stakeholders to advance social or environmental impacts with clear contribution.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.3c.1

The multi-stakeholder collaboration:

- a) has a clear purpose and aims to deliver specific, measurable impacts collaboratively.
- b) aims to create a positive impact on society or the environment.

### 2.3c.2

The company makes a clear contribution to the multi-stakeholder collaboration.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.3c.1] The multi-stakeholder collaboration includes stakeholder(s) that are external to the company.

[2.3c.1] When engaging in multi-stakeholder collaboration, particularly at the industry level, the company follows any applicable local and national antitrust laws.

[2.3c.1.b] To determine whether the multi-stakeholder collaboration contributes to a positive impact on society or the environment, the company consults either:

- the outcome sections of the B Lab Standard
- the U.N. Sustainable Development Goals or similar frameworks.

[2.3c.2] Examples of a “clear contribution” to the multi-stakeholder collaboration include:

- providing input or resources
- taking a leadership position in the project (e.g. a chair or project lead)
- being active in working groups
- setting and achieving internal targets related to the project
- integrating the output of the project into the company’s strategy.

[2.3c.2] The company does not meet the sub-requirement if it is either:

- an observer (meaning it attends sessions without contributing)
- a non-contributing signatory of the project.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:

- roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
- how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
