# GACA2.3e (APAC 2.3e) : The company uses thought leadership to drive systemic change towards an equitable, inclusive, and regenerative economy, and has a clear outcome.

Impact Area : Government Affairs & Collective Action (Affaires publiques & action collective). Pages PDF 1230-1233. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**GACA2** : The company works collaboratively to advance collective social or environmental impacts.

**GACA2.3e** : The company uses thought leadership to drive systemic change towards an equitable, inclusive, and regenerative economy, and has a clear outcome.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.3e.1

The thought leadership:

- a) has a clear positive outcome
- b) aims to create a positive impact on society or the environment.

### 2.3e.2

The company is involved in at least:

a ) two thought leadership practices before Year 0

- b) three thought leadership practices for Years 3 and 5.

## Intent

None

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.3e.1] The company’s thought leadership activities relate to either:

- to the company’s work and expertise
- if done by senior leaders, directly to their role at the company.

[2.3e.1] Thought leadership activities by senior leaders that do not relate to their role at the company do not meet this sub-requirement.

[2.3e.1.b] To determine whether the thought leadership contributes to a positive impact on society or the environment, the company consults either:

- the outcome sections of the B Lab Standard
- the U.N. Sustainable Development Goals or similar frameworks.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:

- roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
- how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
