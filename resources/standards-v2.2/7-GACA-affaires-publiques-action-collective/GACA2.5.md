# GACA2.5 (APAC 2.5) : The company takes part in collective action to advance social or environmental impacts.

*Intitulé FR (traduction à relire) : L'entreprise participe à une action collective visant à faire progresser les impacts sociaux ou environnementaux*

Impact Area : Government Affairs & Collective Action (Affaires publiques & action collective). Pages PDF 1210-1212. Échéance : Year 0.

## Requirement

**GACA2** : The company works collaboratively to advance collective social or environmental impacts.

**GACA2.5** : The company takes part in collective action to advance social or environmental impacts.

## Compliance criteria

### 2.5.1

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company takes part in collective action from the five options in this Impact Topic.

- a) GACA2.3a The company mentors others in its industry, profession, or value chain to advance their social or environmental impacts.
- b) GACA2.3b The company contributes to external research to advance social or environmental impacts.
- c) GACA2.3c The company collaborates with multiple stakeholders to advance social or environmental impacts with clear contribution.
- d) GACA2.3d The company promotes public policy to advance social or environmental impacts with clear contribution.
- e) GACA2.3e The company uses thought leadership to drive systemic change towards an equitable, inclusive, and regenerative economy, and has a clear outcome.

### 2.5.2

*Échéance du critère : Year 0 (celle de la sous-exigence)*

The company takes part in at least:

- a) two of the five collective actions before Year 0
- b) four collective actions for Years 3 and 5.

## Intent

To encourage companies to have positive social and environmental impacts and act beyond their own interests. Companies have a key role to play in collective solutions to systemic social and environmental challenges. Embracing collective action is an essential part of being a B Corp.

## Clarifying the compliance criteria

[2.5.1] The company cannot meet this sub-requirement with collective action it uses to meet other sub-requirements in the B Lab Standard. The actions for this sub-requirement are in addition to any collective action used to meet other sub-requirements.

[2.5.1] See the Compliance Criteria under the respective collective action sub-requirements for details.

## Applying the criteria

*Applying the Criteria to Independently Certifying Subsidiaries*

The action takes place within the company. If the company references an out-of-scope corporate group action, it demonstrates:

- roll out, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms
- how the outcomes impact its operations. For any outcomes that are not impactful, the company creates its own actions.
